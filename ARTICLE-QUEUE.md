# What this site covers

Four things. Nothing else.

---

## 1. PLAY'S official announcements

**The core.** New sets, cards, events, campaigns, balance changes, maintenance that matters.

Sources: `dmps.takaratomy.co.jp/news/` and `@dmps_info`.

Report what was posted. Do not report what it means. A hashtag is not membership. Read **every**
official page on a subject — a sale-day notice can omit its own contents and point back to an
advance notice.

---

## 2. Paper TCG official announcements

**Including individual card reveals — they are PLAY'S previews.**

PLAY'S rebuilds the paper game's card pool a few years behind. A card revealed on paper now is a
card PLAY'S players will be playing with later, often with its numbers adjusted. For an English
reader following the digital game, paper reveals are advance notice of their own format.

That changes the filter. Cover reveals, and say what they are:

| Cover | Skip |
|---|---|
| **Individual card reveals** — with the caveat that PLAY'S may adjust them | Retail promotions |
| Hall of Fame revisions | Store campaigns |
| Anything Magic-adjacent | Sleeve and accessory listings |
| Set releases, as calendar entries | Anything requiring a Japanese address to act on |

**The caveat matters.** A card is not guaranteed to reach PLAY'S, and when it does, cost, power and
text are frequently changed — the digital game rebalances rather than ports. Write "this exists on
paper", not "this is coming to PLAY'S".

Sources: `dm.takaratomy.co.jp`, `@t2duema`, and `/rule/regulation/` every time regardless of what
the front page says.

### The Japan-only line, and the two times it has been crossed

**"Anything requiring a Japanese address to act on" sits in the Skip column**, and the
`神アート` premium art sets are exactly that: Takara Tomy Mall only, made to order, no supply
after the window shuts. Two have been written up anyway, and the reason has to be stated or the
rule means nothing.

- **The Magic sets, 11 September.** Covered under *anything Magic-adjacent*, which is in the Cover
  column, and the article says in its own words that it is listed because a Magic player would want
  to know it exists, not because it is actionable.
- **The PLAY'S sets, 31 October.** Covered because **the subject is PLAY'S** — the characters on the
  cards are from the game this site is about, and one set is built on a promotion this site already
  has a page on.

**So the test is the subject, not the shelf.** A Mall exclusive about PLAY'S or about Magic is
covered and carries a plain statement that it cannot be bought from outside Japan. A Mall exclusive
about neither — sleeves, a storage box, a paper-only product line — is still skipped. If that line
has to be crossed a third time for a reason not on this list, write the reason here first.

## 3. Tournament results

**Yes.** Sanctioned events publish deck usage and winning lists. This is real data with a sample
size, not an opinion.

REIGN DMPS VERTEX CUP runs roughly weekly; Kamigame publishes the metagame breakdown afterwards,
credited to the organiser.

What to write:
- Usage percentages and field size, as published
- Whole-field versus upper-bracket split — they differ, and the difference is the story
- **A one-line description of each deck**: civilizations, deck type, key card, build and operating
  difficulty. Classification, not strategy
- Winning decklist and pilot

What not to write: how to pilot anything. That is the Japanese guides' work.

## 4. What Japanese players are talking about

**The most valuable thing here, and the easiest to get wrong.**

Nobody else is translating this. English readers can find a ban list; they cannot find out that a
card is making people quit, or that a format is being called a grind.

### The limit that was not a limit

**This section said until 1 November 2026 that X search "returns 6–11 posts and stops", that
scrolling does not load more, and that it had been "tested repeatedly". That was wrong.** It pages
properly with real input scrolling — one query reached **40 posts over a 1 hour 46 minute window**
and was still climbing when the test stopped. The cap was an artefact of scripted scrolling, the
same artefact `WATCH-TASK.md` had already diagnosed for the timeline; the search entry was simply
never re-run after the fix. The method is in `WATCH-TASK.md`.

**What still holds.** A search returns what the index gives for that term over that window. It is
not a census of players, it is not weighted, and a term people reach for when annoyed will return
annoyed posts. **So "players are unhappy" is still off the table** — not because the sample is
eleven, but because no search can support a claim about players in general.

**What is newly allowed.** Counting over a stated window, with the query and the window named:
"of the 40 posts this query returned between 22:45 and 00:31, nine raise the card selection". That
is a measurement, and it was not available while the cap was believed in.

### What to write instead

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Quote the engagement figure: "a post with 680 likes says" | "Several independent posts say" |
| State the count seen: "of the 11 posts checked, 4 raise" | "Most posts raise" |
| Report a single large post as a single large post | Extrapolate one post into a trend |

**A post with 331,000 views is a fact on its own.** That is the sample-size problem solved rather
than dodged — the reach is the evidence, not the count of similar posts.

Never quote individuals by name. Paraphrase.

## 5. Metagame data

**Numbers exist, but not the ones people expect.**

| Source | What it gives | Sample |
|---|---|---|
| BEANS daily survey | Ranked deck distribution, ND and AD separately | ~400 games/day |
| Kamigame ranked data | Same, split into all players and top 500 | ~100 games |
| Tournament breakdowns | Submitted decklists, cleanest data available | 250–320 decks |
| Kamigame deck guides | Full 40-card lists, build cost in DMP, tier, deck type | — |

**No win rates exist anywhere.** Not officially, not from third parties. Every claim that a deck is
"strong" is inference from play rate and placement.

Deck lists and build costs are published as **images**, which means reading them requires opening
the page and enlarging the graphic. They are worth the effort: a 40-card list with a build cost in
DMP is the most concretely useful thing this site can carry for someone deciding what to make.

Always state which source, which date, and which bracket. The tournament and ladder pictures
disagree — [by six weeks of drift, in the last case](2026-09-08-the-three-decks.html).

## Not covered: tier lists

**Dropped deliberately.**

Tier placements are one editorial team's judgement, stated as such on their own page. Translating
them means republishing someone's opinion and then inheriting the obligation to track every revision
of it — several times a week, indefinitely.

Tournament data does the same job better: it is measured, it has a sample size, it arrives on a
schedule, and it is nobody's opinion.

Existing tier articles stay up as dated snapshots. No new ones.

---

## Order when several are available

1. Corrections to anything already published
2. PLAY'S official announcements
3. Tournament results
4. Community topics
5. Metagame data, when a survey or tournament breakdown lands
6. Paper TCG — card reveals included, framed as what exists on paper today

Publishing nothing beats publishing a correction.
