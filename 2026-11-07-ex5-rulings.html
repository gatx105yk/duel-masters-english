The new set’s awkward cases, settled before it reached shops

DM26-EX5 reached shops today. On 2 November, five days before that, the official デュエチューブ channel put up a seven-minute video in which the developers go through the cards in it whose text is hard to read, and give rulings.

On 6 November the publisher also put six entries in its official Q&A for the set, in writing. The two sources overlap but do not match: the Q&A is the firmer text and covers things the video skips, while two of the cards the video rules on have no Q&A entry at all. Each ruling below says which one it rests on.

What counts as a card underneath an evolution creature

The set’s two new evolution creatures are conditioned on the number of cards that end up beneath them — a condition that appears nowhere else in the official card database, which this site looked at separately today. That makes “how many cards is that, exactly” the first thing worth settling, and the official Q&A settles it in writing.

The condition, in the publisher’s own words

Official Q&A 49602, dated 6 November 2026
クリーチャー１体以上、かつ、それらに含まれるカードの合計が２枚以上ならその上に置けます。

One or more creatures, and the cards they are made of adding up to two or more. The Q&A then lists what that allows, and the list is more useful than the rule:

・クリーチャーや「シンカライズ」を持つタマシード、合計２つ
Two of anything between creatures and シンカライズ Tamaseeds.

・進化クリーチャーや《DARK MATERIAL COMPLEX》など、下にカードが１枚以上置かれたクリーチャー(下のカードはクリーチャーでなくても構いません)
A single creature is enough if it already has at least one card under it — and that card does not have to be a creature.

・《邪眼左神エンドレス》《邪眼右神デリート》など、２枚でリンクしているゴッド
Two linked Gods.

・«Volzeos-Balamord»や«勝利の頂上 ヴォルグ・イソレイト6th»のような、２枚以上のカードでリンクしている１体のクリーチャー
One creature whose face is two or more linked cards.

What is not enough

Official Q&A 49602
«爆熱DX バトライ武神»はカード３枚分の大きさですが、１枚のカードですので、それ１枚の上には進化できません。

Three panels wide, one card. You cannot evolve onto it on its own. Physical size is not the measure; the number of cards is.

The Q&A is inconsistent about its own brackets: within this one answer some card names sit in 《 》 and others, including this one, in « ». The quotations above keep whichever the source used.

また、オーラの付いたクリーチャーやクロスギアがクロスされているクリーチャーも、オーラやクロスギアは進化元のカードではないため、それ単体の上には進化できません。

An Aura or a Cross Gear attached to a creature is not one of the cards underneath it, so a lone creature wearing one still counts as a single card and is not enough on its own.

The video makes the same point about a ウエポン, which the Q&A does not mention. That one rests on the video alone.

Non-creature cards underneath are fine

Official Q&A 49601, dated 6 November 2026

Asked directly whether 《無限龍王ボルシャック・ライジングNEX》 can be evolved onto an evolution creature that has non-creature cards such as a Tamaseed underneath, the answer is yes:

このクリーチャーを進化クリーチャーの上に置く際、その進化元のカードはクリーチャーでなくても問題ありません。

The Q&A notes that the same applies to 《暗黒神羅凰ゼロ・フェニックス》.

NEO creatures count as non-evolution creatures

Felix Misery

《フェリックス・ミザリィ》 · DM26-EX5
このクリーチャーが攻撃する時、自分の山札の上から２枚を墓地に置く。その後、闇のコスト６以下の、進化ではないクリーチャーを１体、自分の墓地から出してもよい。

When it attacks, two cards go from the top of your deck to your graveyard, and then you may put a darkness creature of cost six or less that is not an evolution creature out of your graveyard into play.

A NEO creature counts as one of those, the video says — it is not an evolution creature for the purpose of that line.

And when you put one out this way you may put it out as an evolution creature, stacked on top of one of your creatures, rather than on its own.

There is no official Q&A for this card. Both rulings above rest on the video alone.

A graveyard of exactly sixteen

Daigoku no Fuusatsu Dias Z

《大獄の封殺ディアス Z》 · DM26-EX5
自分の墓地にカードが16枚以上なければ、このクリーチャーは攻撃できない。

It also has an attack trigger that may move four cards from a player’s graveyard to the bottom of their deck — and that player may be you.

So: your graveyard is on exactly sixteen, you attack, and the trigger takes four of your own cards out of it, leaving twelve. The attack is not called off. The requirement is read when the attack is declared, and the graveyard dropping below sixteen afterwards does not undo it.

When a tap ability may be used

Gensou Yousei Katyua

《幻想妖精カチュア》 · DM26-EX5 — a reprint
このクリーチャーで攻撃するかわりに、タップしてその{t} 能力を使ってもよい。

The database writes the tap symbol as {t}; on the card itself it is the symbol, not the braces.

Instead of attacking with it, you may tap it to use its tap ability. The video gives three rulings on when that is allowed.

It is an activated ability used during the attack step, and only while カチュア is not summoning-sick.

Any point in that step will do. You may use it after another creature has already attacked, or the moment the step begins.

But not mid-resolution. Like ハイパー化, it cannot be used while another effect is resolving, or during a creature’s attack.

The card is not new to this set — the database lists it in three earlier products as well.

There is no official Q&A for this card either. These three rulings rest on the video alone.

One topic this page is leaving alone

The video also covers 《超神星マーキュリー・ギガブリザード》, saying its text has been changed from the version players will remember so that it now behaves the same way as two other existing cards. Those two names were not legible enough on screen to reproduce, and this site does not publish card names read off video frames. The ruling is noted here so that anyone who needs it knows where to look; the names are not guessed at.

The official Q&A carries six entries for this set, and three of them are about cards this page does not cover — one on 《超神星ヴァルカン・アンチャンス》 and two on 《闇鎧亜ジャック・アルカディアス / 執拗なる鎧亜の牢獄》. They are numbered 49603, 49604 and 49605 and are reachable from each card’s own database page. Naming them here is not a summary of them; it is so that nobody reads this page as the whole set of rulings.

Sources, and which one each ruling rests on Two official sources, and they do not cover the same ground.

The Q&A. Six entries at dm.takaratomy.co.jp/rule/qa/, numbered 49601 to 49606 and all dated 6 November 2026, reachable from each card’s database page. Everything on this page shown in a quotation block with a Q&A number beside it is the publisher’s own written wording, copied from that page.

The video. 【ルール解説】…「電脳対戦 -WAR OF PLAY'S-」…【裁定】 on the official デュエチューブ-DM公式- channel, published 2 November 2026 at 18:00 JST, 439 seconds long. It covers 《フェリックス・ミザリィ》 and 《幻想妖精カチュア》, which have no Q&A at all, so for those the video is the only source there is.

Rulings attributed to the video are summaries, not quotations. It puts its rulings on screen as Japanese text, but that text can only be read off video frames, and this site has already shipped two misquotations that came from reading too fast. Where a ruling rests on the video it is given in English as what it says, and anyone checking should expect the sense to match and the wording not to.

Card text is different again. Every passage of Japanese card text above was taken from that card’s own page in the official card database at dm.takaratomy.co.jp/card/, not from the video, and the card names were confirmed the same way.

This page makes no claim about PLAY’S. These are paper rulings on paper cards. Nothing published says how any of it will work in the digital game.
Rebuilt 11 November on the official Q&A, which existed when this page was written As first published on 7 November, this page said its rulings could only be summarised because they existed only as text on screen in a video. That was wrong about the sources. The publisher's official Q&A already carried six entries for this set, all dated 6 November — the day before this page went up — and they were linked from each card's own database page, which this page had already been reading for card text. They were simply not looked for.

What changed as a result: the evolution-source section is now the Q&A's own wording rather than a summary; Cross Gear was missing entirely and is now covered; 《爆熱DX バトライ武神》 is named, because the Q&A names it in text and the earlier refusal to name it was about reading names off video frames; and a ruling the video does not give — that the cards underneath need not be creatures — has been added.

The headline changed too. It read “settled on screen five days early”, which described the video. With the Q&A leading, the rulings were settled in writing a day before release rather than on screen five days before, so the headline now says only that they were settled before the set reached shops.

Two of the quotations were wrong when this rebuild first went up, later the same day. The Q&A writes some card names in « » and others in 《 》, and the two that use « » — the Volzeos-Balamord line and the 爆熱DX バトライ武神 line — were silently normalised to 《 》 when they were copied in. That is a change to quoted text, which is the one thing a quotation may not do, and it is the third time this site has shipped a misquotation. All quotations on this page and on the keyword page were then checked character by character against their sources; these two were the only ones that differed.
