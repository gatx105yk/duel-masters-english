The new set’s awkward cases, settled on screen five days early

DM26-EX5 reached shops today. On 2 November, five days before that, the official デュエチューブ channel put up a seven-minute video in which the developers go through the cards in it whose text is hard to read, and give rulings.

Four topics. The rulings appear as text on screen rather than only being spoken, which is unusual and makes them easier to rely on — though, as the note at the foot of this page explains, it is also why this page summarises them rather than quoting them.

What counts as a card underneath an evolution creature

The set’s two new evolution creatures are conditioned on the number of cards that end up beneath them — a condition that appears nowhere else in the official card database, which this site looked at separately today. That makes “how many cards is that, exactly” the first thing worth settling, and the video settles it in three parts.

A creature built from several linked cards goes under as one bundle

Where a creature’s face is made up of more than one card locked together, the whole thing is placed underneath as a single stack rather than being counted card by card.

A card that is physically several panels wide is still one card. The video names two existing creatures that are printed three panels across and says each counts as one. They are not named here, for the reason given at the foot of the page.

Weapons and Auras do not count at all

Neither a ウエポン nor an オーラ adds to the number, and the video gives a different reason for each.

A ウエポン is not one of the cards the creature is made of in the first place.

An オーラ is one of them — but when the creature is evolved, the Aura moves up and attaches to the new creature on top instead of staying underneath. Either way it is not among the cards being counted.

NEO creatures count as non-evolution creatures

Felix Misery

《フェリックス・ミザリィ》 · DM26-EX5
このクリーチャーが攻撃する時、自分の山札の上から２枚を墓地に置く。その後、闇のコスト６以下の、進化ではないクリーチャーを１体、自分の墓地から出してもよい。

When it attacks, two cards go from the top of your deck to your graveyard, and then you may put a darkness creature of cost six or less that is not an evolution creature out of your graveyard into play.

A NEO creature counts as one of those, the video says — it is not an evolution creature for the purpose of that line.

And when you put one out this way you may put it out as an evolution creature, stacked on top of one of your creatures, rather than on its own.

A graveyard of exactly sixteen

Daigoku no Fuusatsu Dias Z

《大獄の封殺ディアス Z》 · DM26-EX5
自分の墓地にカードが16枚以上なければ、このクリーチャーは攻撃できない。

It also has an attack trigger that may move four cards from a player’s graveyard to the bottom of their deck — and that player may be you.

So: your graveyard is on exactly sixteen, you attack, and the trigger takes four of your own cards out of it, leaving twelve. The attack is not called off. The requirement is read when the attack is declared, and the graveyard dropping below sixteen afterwards does not undo it.

When a tap ability may be used

Gensou Yousei Katyua

《幻想妖精カチュア》 · DM26-EX5 — a reprint
このクリーチャーで攻撃するかわりに、タップしてその{t} 能力を使ってもよい。

The database writes the tap symbol as {t}; on the card itself it is the symbol, not the braces.

Instead of attacking with it, you may tap it to use its tap ability. The video gives three rulings on when that is allowed.

It is an activated ability used during the attack step, and only while カチュア is not summoning-sick.

Any point in that step will do. You may use it after another creature has already attacked, or the moment the step begins.

But not mid-resolution. Like ハイパー化, it cannot be used while another effect is resolving, or during a creature’s attack.

The card is not new to this set — the database lists it in three earlier products as well.

One topic this page is leaving alone

The video also covers 《超神星マーキュリー・ギガブリザード》, saying its text has been changed from the version players will remember so that it now behaves the same way as two other existing cards. Those two names were not legible enough on screen to reproduce, and this site does not publish card names read off video frames. The ruling is noted here so that anyone who needs it knows where to look; the names are not guessed at.

Sources, and why nothing here is in quotation marks The video: 【ルール解説】…「電脳対戦 -WAR OF PLAY'S-」…【裁定】 on the official デュエチューブ-DM公式- channel, published 2 November 2026 at 18:00 JST, 439 seconds long. The paper account @t2duema posted it the following day, not the day it went up.

The rulings above are summaries, not translations of quotations. The video puts them on screen as Japanese text, but that text can only be read off video frames, and this site has already shipped two misquotations that came from reading too fast. Rather than present a transcription as exact, each ruling is given here in English as what it says. Anyone checking this page against the video should expect the sense to match and the wording not to.

Card text is different. Every passage of Japanese card text above was taken from that card’s own page in the official card database at dm.takaratomy.co.jp/card/ on 7 November 2026, not from the video, and the card names were confirmed the same way.

This page makes no claim about PLAY’S. These are paper rulings on paper cards. Nothing published says how any of it will work in the digital game.
