The art line that was printing Magic cards has turned to PLAY'S

Two more 神アート絆 sets went up for pre-order on the evening of 30 October. Both are built on デュエプレ — the digital game — where the first two in the line were built on Magic: The Gathering.

Five cards in each, and every card is listed with a PLAY’S character beside it. Pre-orders close on 1 December.

What was announced

Kami Art Kizuna — 神アート絆 — is the collaboration half of Takara Tomy’s premium art line. The Mall’s own description of the line splits it in two: 「デュエル・マスターズの世界観をアートに昇華した「極」。他作品などとのコラボ展開を行う「絆」。」 — 極 for Duel Masters’ own world, 絆 for work with something else. This site covered the first two, and described the line then as made-to-order product with new artwork on existing cards, sold direct rather than through shops.

What this page is not telling you

Three gaps, named rather than filled Whether these ten cards already exist on paper is not established here. The listings do not say, and the official card search could not be made to answer it: a search for a card that certainly exists on paper returned the same empty result as a search for one of these, so neither result means anything and no claim is built on either.

No readings are given for the Japanese names. The names are copied from the listings exactly. The listings do not give readings, and this site does not invent them.

No card text, costs or powers. Those are legible only from the product images, which carry 「※画像は全て開発中のものです。実際の商品とは異なる場合があります。」 — development images, subject to change. They are not read off here.

For English speakers

You cannot buy this Takara Tomy Mall ships within Japan, and made to order means there is no supply after the window closes on 1 December. At 7,700 yen for five cards this is a collector’s product, not a way to get playable cards.

It is here because the subject is PLAY’S. The Mall lists four sets in the whole 絆 line, and the other two are 絆-01 and 絆-02, the Magic pair, both now showing 予約期間終了 and released late January 2027. So on what the Mall shows, these are the first in the line to take their subject from the digital game — a delisted set would not appear there, which is the limit of that check.
