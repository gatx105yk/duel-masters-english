## Angle

Week three's headline was that four decks led and the one that led most finished lowest.
Week four's is narrower and stronger: **the week where the readings stopped meaning a week.**
Both ends of the series' sample sizes, two decks resetting both their extremes inside six days,
and the biggest single-day swing yet — and underneath it a season reset on the 16th and a card
change on the 22nd, so neither end of the week is comparable to the other.

Working title: **Week four: the sample ran from 440 games to 84, and two decks posted both their
extremes inside it**

Do not conclude the format is volatile in itself. At 84 games a point is under one game, and the
week's two largest moves both belong to the day either side of the smallest sample in it. The
honest reading is that **the survey got noisier, and the decks moved inside that noise.**
