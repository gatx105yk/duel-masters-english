Set 37 is running two opposite ideas at once

At least seventeen cards have been shown so far, and the ones whose text has been published fall into three lines, two of which share a shape and one of which does the opposite. One empties your hand and spends your own shields. The other stacks cards up under a two-cost field until it can cast a ninety-nine cost spell for free.

Effects are described below in plain English; the official posts are embedded so you can see the cards themselves.

Line one: Fire, and running out on purpose

Every Fire card shown pays you for having less — fewer cards in hand, fewer shields in front of you.

Balga Raiza

《龍星装者 “B-我”ライザ》 · 我 is read Balga · Super Rare
Type CreatureRace Dragon Guild / Beat Jockey Cost 8Power 8000
SympathyOne less to cast for each Beat Jockey you control. It cannot drop below one, since the card is single-civilization. Speed AttackerAttacks the turn it arrives. Double BreakerBreaks two shields. G・G・GWhile your hand is one card or fewer, your other creatures all gain Speed Attacker. On attackWhenever any of your creatures attacks, flip the top card of your deck. A Beat Jockey or Dragon costing no more than the attacker may go straight into play; anything else goes to the bottom.

Flow "GIG" Roll

《フロウ“GIG”ロール》 · Uncommon
Type NEO CreatureRace Beat Jockey Cost 7Power 2000+
NEO EvolutionStacks onto a Fire creature, or is played as an ordinary creature. On summonYou may bin one of your own shields to take five off the cost. ConditionalIf any player is on two shields or fewer, it gets +4000 power and Double Breaker.

Seven mana becomes two if you are willing to take the hit — and the shield you spent is what switches on the bonus. The cost and the payoff are the same action. The condition reads any player, so an opponent breaking your shields does the job as well.

Sukebo Churis

《スケボー・チュリス》 · Common
Type CreatureRace Beat Jockey Cost 3Power 2000
On playChoose one: discard a card and draw two, or destroy an opposing creature of power 2000 or less.

The small one that makes the rest work. Discarding to draw is how a Fire deck reaches an empty hand deliberately — and an empty hand is the condition everything else is waiting on.

A fourth Fire card, revealed separately through Corocoro, takes the same idea further: free to cast if it is the only card you are holding, then a board wipe. Its own page is here.

Line two: Darkness, and stacking up to ninety-nine

The cards shown on Grand Prix day do the opposite. Nothing is spent — it is banked.

Shinsekai (Grand Zero)

《卐 新世壊 卐》 · read Grand Zero · Super Rare
Type Mugetsu FieldCost 2 Power —
BankAfter you cast one of your Madougu spells, you may tuck it under this field instead of sending it to the graveyard, and draw a card. OverrideYou may cast Madougu and Dolsuzak spells even through effects that stop you casting. FragileIf the field leaves the battle zone, everything stacked under it is binned. Gate 99At the end of your turn, with four or more cards under it, once per game: cast a Water spell costing 99 or less from your hand or graveyard, free.

Ga Ryumizu

《月下卐壊 ガ・リュミーズ 卐》 · Super Rare
Type SpellRace Dolsuzak Cost 99
EffectSummon up to four Dolsuzak from your hand or graveyard without paying for them. EffectTake an extra turn after this one — unless this already is one.
The ninety-nine is not decoration The field unlocks a Water spell costing 99 or less. This spell costs exactly 99. The number exists so that these two cards find each other, and no honest deck ever pays it.

So the sequence reads: two mana to place the field, spells tucked underneath as you cast them, and on the turn the fourth one lands, four free creatures and another turn. That is the shape of the Darkness line.

Two more from the same day

Melavolgal and Shiten no Sabaki 《紫天ノ裁キ》 were shown the same day. Both belong to the Darkness line; the first is a Dolsuzak, described in its post as destroying without regard for sides. I have not read either card's text and am not describing it.

A note on that first name: the official post and the main Japanese guide site spell it differently — 凶鬼卐号 against 凶鬼09号. Until the card itself has been read, this page does not pick one.

Line three: Water, and a new keyword

Two Water cards arrived on 15 September, and with them an ability the game has not had before.

Zenoshark / Crystal Memory

《水晶の記録 ゼノシャーク／クリスタル・メモリー》 · Very Rare
Type TwinpactRace Mutopia Creature 3 / 1000Spell 4
Smash BurstWhen the creature attacks, cast this card's own spell half for free. Spell halfShield Trigger. Name a number, then Super Search your deck for a card of that cost and take it to hand.
"Super Search" is not the same as searching PLAY'S does not let you look through your whole deck. A search shows you up to three distinct cards matching the condition, chosen at random from those that qualify; a Super Search shows five. You pick from what you are shown.

In practice it is often a clean tutor — few decks run six or more different cards at the same cost, so naming a number usually surfaces what you wanted. But it is a cap, not a guarantee, and the paper version of this spell has no such limit.
Smash Burst changes what a Twinpact is Until now a Twinpact made you pick a half — creature or spell, not both. This keyword lets one card be both: a three-cost body that fires a four-cost tutor every time it attacks, and that still works as a Shield Trigger sitting in defence.

Clanvia

《不夜城艦 クランヴィア》 · Super Rare
Type CreatureRace Mutopia Cost 4Power 4000
On playReveal the top three cards of your deck; every spell among them goes underneath this creature, the rest to the graveyard. BankAfter you cast a spell from hand, you may tuck it under a Clanvia instead of binning it. On attackBin a spell from underneath whose civilization matches a card in your mana zone, and cast it for free.

Which is the Darkness field's idea again, on a creature. Spells accumulate underneath; attacking spends them. Two different civilizations have now been given the same shape.

This is the twenty-two everyone was asking about A spell nobody casts honestly. Clanvia banks spells and casts one free when it attacks — so the ship turns into two dead creatures and a 22222-power Speed Attacker arriving at once. The video asking how anyone is meant to use a 22-cost card was about this, and the answer was published the same week.

Seventeenth card, revealed today

Gilles Tocqueville

《ジル・トクヴィル》 · Uncommon · PLAY'S original
Type CreatureRace Mafi Gang Cost 3Power 1000
Shield TriggerCast free from a broken shield. BlockerMay block an attacker. On playLook at the top three cards of your deck; bin one and put the rest back underneath in random order. DrawbackIf it attacks, it destroys itself afterwards.

A defensive card that fills your graveyard. Three mana, blocks, arrives free off a broken shield, and puts a card in the bin every time it lands. The self-destruction clause says plainly what it is not for: this is not a card you attack with.

A guess, flagged as one The Darkness line in this set banks spells under a two-cost field and can cast the payoff from the graveyard. A three-cost blocker that bins a card on arrival looks like it belongs in that deck.

Nothing has been announced tying them together, and the card names no archetype. Treat the connection as a reading of two card texts, not a fact.

What players are making of it

Paraphrased and anonymised from twenty posts read across two passes. Not a measurement of sentiment — twenty posts cannot be. One figure below is counted rather than sampled, but it is still a forecast.

The ship is the one people are talking about Most of the twelve concern Clanvia. Several are links to video breakdowns published within a day, and one person says the card is why they opened the game again. Another notes, drily, that it is a card for making your opponent uncomfortable — a reputation it carries over from paper.
The new keyword landed well Smash Burst is described as a strong ability in the same breath as an existing one it resembles, and one post is already pairing it with a card in the current pool.
The open question got answered A video asked outright how anyone would cast a particular high-cost spell. It was the 22-cost half of Money King, and the answer — Clanvia — was already public. Players worked the pairing out within a day of the second card appearing.
A number — but a forecast, not a measurement A Japanese community site runs polls on new cards before they are playable. Money King currently sits at 3.00 out of 5, which its scale calls average.

That is a poll of expectations, not a record of performance. Nobody voting has played with the card. It is a larger sample than the posts above it, and it is still opinion — the same kind of thing, counted. The measured numbers on this site come from games that were actually played, and these are not those.
And a rules question nobody has answered One post links the paper game's own rules Q&A and asks whether Money King will arrive tapped when the spell half puts it into play, as the paper ruling would have it. No official answer yet. If it does arrive tapped, the Speed Attacker on it matters a great deal less.

What the split means

Two of the three lines win by stacking cards underneath something and spending them later. The third wins by having nothing left at all. Seventeen cards in, this is the clearest thing about the set

The Darkness field banks spells under itself; the Water ship banks spells under a creature. Those are the same idea given to two civilizations. Fire is the one running the other way.

And none of them is what the set is named after. The headline cards are Nature — five reprints of a 2018 paper creature, built on stacking dragons under a Phoenix Field and casting off them. Those are covered here.

Four civilizations, three or four distinct plans depending on how you count. Worth holding loosely: seventeen cards is a small fraction of a set, and the shape may not survive the rest of it.
