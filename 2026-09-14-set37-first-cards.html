Set 37 is running two opposite ideas at once

The set lands on 24 September. At least thirty-three cards have been shown so far, and the ones whose text has been published fall into three lines, two of which share a shape and one of which does the opposite. One empties your hand and spends your own shields. The other stacks cards up under a two-cost field until it can cast a ninety-nine cost spell for free.

The set lands on 24 September, and the full list is out: ninety base cards, four gold-treatment reprints and twelve secrets. Below are the thirty-four that were revealed one at a time, described in full — and under them, what the complete list settles about the shape of the set.

Effects are described below in plain English; the official posts are embedded so you can see the cards themselves.

Line one: Fire, and running out on purpose

Every Fire card shown pays you for having less — fewer cards in hand, fewer shields in front of you.

The small one that makes the rest work. Discarding to draw is how a Fire deck reaches an empty hand deliberately — and an empty hand is the condition everything else is waiting on.

A fourth Fire card, revealed separately through Corocoro, takes the same idea further: free to cast if it is the only card you are holding, then a board wipe. Its own page is here.

Line two: Darkness, and stacking up to ninety-nine

The cards shown on Grand Prix day do the opposite. Nothing is spent — it is banked.

The ninety-nine is not decoration The field unlocks a Water spell costing 99 or less. This spell costs exactly 99. The number exists so that these two cards find each other, and no honest deck ever pays it.

Confirmed since: the publisher's own gameplay showcase ran exactly this line — the field's gate casting the ninety-nine spell.

So the sequence reads: two mana to place the field, spells tucked underneath as you cast them, and on the turn the fourth one lands, four free creatures and another turn. That is the shape of the Darkness line.

Two more from the same day

Melavolgal and Shiten no Sabaki 《紫天ノ裁キ》 were shown the same day. Both belong to the Darkness line; the first is a Dolsuzak, described in its post as destroying without regard for sides. I have not read either card's text and am not describing it.

A note on that first name: the official post and the main Japanese guide site spell it differently — 凶鬼卐号 against 凶鬼09号. Until the card itself has been read, this page does not pick one.

Line three: Water, and a new keyword

Two Water cards arrived on 15 September, and with them an ability the game has not had before.

Zenoshark / Crystal Memory

《水晶の記録 ゼノシャーク／クリスタル・メモリー》 · Very Rare
Type TwinpactRace Mutopia Creature 3 / 1000Spell 4
Smash BurstWhen the creature attacks, cast this card's own spell half for free. Spell halfShield Trigger. Name a number, then Super Search your deck for a card of that cost and take it to hand.
"Super Search" is not the same as searching PLAY'S does not let you look through your whole deck. A search shows you up to three distinct cards matching the condition, chosen at random from those that qualify; a Super Search shows five. You pick from what you are shown.

In practice it is often a clean tutor — few decks run six or more different cards at the same cost, so naming a number usually surfaces what you wanted. But it is a cap, not a guarantee, and the paper version of this spell has no such limit.
Smash Burst changes what a Twinpact is Until now a Twinpact made you pick a half — creature or spell, not both. This keyword lets one card be both: a three-cost body that fires a four-cost tutor every time it attacks, and that still works as a Shield Trigger sitting in defence.

The Madougu spells that make the Darkness engine run

Three cheap Water spells were shown on the official video channel on Grand Prix day. They are the fuel for the two-cost field above — every one you cast goes underneath it.

CardCostWhat it does
Daju Gonpadu 堕呪ゴンパドゥ2Look at the top three, take one, rest to the bottom.
Daju Gyapudu 堕呪ギャプドゥ2Draw one. Super Shield Trigger: if it fires that way, no opposing creature can attack or block.
Daju Airvo 堕呪エアヴォ4Shield Trigger. Bounce anything costing seven or less.
Super Shield Trigger is a different keyword An ordinary Shield Trigger fires from any broken shield. A Super Shield Trigger has a further condition attached and does something larger when it is met — here, shutting down the opponent's whole board for a turn. Most of the time you cast the card normally and get the small effect.

The point of the cheap two-cost ones is speed. The field wants four spells underneath it before it does anything, so the deck runs ten or more cards that cost two and dig. Getting the field down by turn three is the whole opening.

And one of them shows the usual weakening not happening A recurring pattern when cards cross over is that they become opponent-only — a bounce spell that could hit anything in paper can only hit their side here.

The four-cost bounce above was not restricted that way. It can return your own cards, which matters because the field is a card you sometimes want back. It did lose something else, though: it now targets elements rather than cards, so the spells stacked under a returned field come back with it rather than going to the graveyard.

And Light gets a blocker that fetches blockers

Welkius

《闘門の精霊ウェルキウス》 · Super Rare
Type CreatureRace Angel Command Cost 9Power 7500
BodyBlocker, Double Breaker. TriggerOn arrival, and again whenever one of your creatures wins a battle: draw a card, then you may put a Blocker from your hand into play.

Nine mana, and no deck will pay it. A nine-cost Light Blocker is exactly what Heaven's Gate cheats into play for free — and this one, on arrival, puts another Blocker out behind it.

Which changes what that deck is doing The Nature/Light preconstructed deck currently plays the Gate as a ceiling: two big blockers, then win from behind them. This makes the Gate a chain instead. Cheat this in, it fetches a third; block with it and win the battle, it fetches a fourth.

The seven-card battle zone cap starts to matter at that point — a full board turns the next one into a discard.

The set is retelling a comic from 2018

Messer Schmitt

《メッサー・シュミット》 · Rare
Civ NatureRace Spriggan / Grand Sect Cost 8Power 8000+
BodyMach Fighter, Double Breaker, and +4000 power while fighting. UnusualYou may summon it out of your mana zone, not only from hand. On winningDraw a card. If it is the first battle it has won this turn, you may drag another of the opponent's creatures into a fight with it.
Three cards in this set are the same scene In the 2018 comic that ran alongside the paper release, Messer Schmitt is a legendary hunter who has been brainwashed, and he fights alongside the Darkness factory boss — the Twinpact further up this page.

He is beaten by the Jokers hero, and with his dying words hands over the power that turns that hero into his upgraded form — the creature shown in this set's teaser trailer.

Georg, Messer Schmitt and the upgraded Jokers hero are all in set 37. The set is not a pile of reprints from a period; it is a specific story, cast as cards.

Which is a thing English-speaking players have no access to. The comic has never been published in English, so the connective tissue between these cards is invisible unless somebody points at it.

And the card the set is named after wins the game outright

Minogami

《始虹帝 ミノガミ》 · Master · the “Nijitei” of the set’s title
Race Grand SectCost 5Power 5000+
Mach FighterAttacks creatures the turn it arrives. On attackReveal the top three of your deck; every Twinpact among them goes to your mana zone, the rest to the bottom. +5000 per card banked, for the turn. Muteki NijiteiWhen you would lose, or the opponent would win: if you have cast five or more spell halves of Twinpact creatures this turn while leaving those creatures in play — you win the game instead. If you have not, put eight cards from your mana zone under your deck and carry on.
Read that in the right order This is an alternate win condition first and a safety net second. The official summary described the fallback — survive by burying eight mana — and that is the half that fires when you have not done the work. Do the work and the same trigger simply ends the game in your favour.

The work is Rinfinity, the keyword on the five reprinted Nature creatures that share a name: cast a Twinpact’s spell half while the creature stays on the board. Five of those in a turn, and the loss you were about to take becomes a win.

Which also explains the Twinpacts everywhere in this set. They are not a convenience — they are the win condition’s raw material.

"New" here means new to this game, not to the game The official account has announced three of these as new abilities — the ones in bold. Both of those are keywords that already existed in the paper game in 2018; what is new is that PLAY'S now has them.

The other two are printed on cards without any such announcement. They may be equally new, or they may be wordings this game already had and I have not seen before. This page does not know which, and says so rather than guessing.
Smash Burst is a cycle, not four loose cards Look at what the spell halves are: each one is an existing card. Crystal Memory, Ghost Touch, Super Eternal Spark and the Jokers spell are all cards the game already had.

So the pattern is a new creature welded to a reprint, one per civilization. All four keywords are in the glossary.

Another Baraghiara — but not one of the five

Bōmeiten Baraghiara / Gaia's Awakening

《暴命天 バラギアラ／ガイアの目覚め》 · Rare
Type TwinpactRace Grand Sect / Hazard Creature 9 / 14000Spell 8
CreatureMach Fighter, Triple Breaker, and every creature in the game breaks one extra shield. SpellShield Trigger. Each player sends two of their own creatures to their mana zone.
It is a different card that shares half a name The five announced at the Grand Prix are 天地命動 バラギアラ — one named category, Master Hazard, 9 for 17000, and all five carry Rinfinity.

This is 暴命天 バラギアラ: a different name, a different race line, 9 for 14000, and no Rinfinity.

That cuts it off from the set's win condition twice over. Without Rinfinity you cannot cast its spell half while the creature stays in play, so it never counts towards Minogami's five. And because Rinfinity on the real five counts your Tenchi Meidō Baraghiara specifically, this card does not even feed them.

Note also what the creature does: "every creature" includes the opponent's. Playing it hands the other player an extra shield break on each of their attackers too.

Something leaves as well, on the same day

New Division rotates when the set arrives New Division holds the six most recent releases. A new one arriving pushes the oldest out, and that happens on 24 September along with everything above.

Those cards do not stop existing — they remain legal in All Division, which rotates nothing. But a New Division deck leaning on the outgoing set stops being legal that morning.

The set going out is DMPP-32, 「正義の煌龍」, from September 2025. A Japanese strategy site publishes the rotation schedule a year at a time and has this one marked for September — with the unnumbered special pack that left in July listed in the same table, which is what makes the count add up.

If you are building in New Division before the 24th, check whether your deck leans on that set. A card database will tell you which release a card came from and which formats it is legal in.

What the full list confirms

The complete contents are published: ninety cards, plus four Ultra Golden reprints and twelve secrets. The thirty-four above were shown one a day; the rest arrived as a block. Most of the remainder is what fills a set out — commons and uncommons that support the archetypes rather than define them — but the cycles they form settle a few things this page had been guessing at.

One cycle worth naming Three Metallica Twinpacts share a naming pattern — 緑知銀, 赤攻銀, 青守銀 — green "knowledge", red "attack", blue "defence". Three, not five — Light and Darkness have no counterpart in this set. Each is a creature welded to a cheap spell, the same construction as the Smash Burst cards.

The set is built almost entirely out of cycles: five of this, six of that, one per colour. That is what a set assembled from old preconstructed decks looks like.

What the full list does not do is change the reading above. The Fire cards still empty your hand, the Darkness and Water cards still bank things, and the win condition still runs on Twinpacts. The extra fifty-five reinforce those lines rather than adding a sixth.

The five headline creatures are secrets The 天地命動 バラギアラ cycle is marked Sec on the official reveals — part of the twelve secrets rather than the ninety. The card the set's win condition depends on is in its rarest slot.
