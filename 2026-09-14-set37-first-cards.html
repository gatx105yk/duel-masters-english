Set 37's first cards all want you to have nothing left

Three cards went up on the official account this evening, one an hour, and a fourth came through Corocoro. All four are Fire, and every one of them pays you for running out of something — an empty hand, or a missing shield.

That is a direction, not a coincidence. The official posts are embedded below; the card text is described rather than copied.

Balga Raiza

Ryūseisōsha "B-Ga" Raiza

《龍星装者 “B-我”ライザ》 · 我 is read Balga · Super Rare
Type Creature Race Dragon Guild / Beat Jockey Cost 8 Power 8000
SympathyCosts less for each Beat Jockey you control. Speed AttackerAttacks the turn it arrives. Double BreakerBreaks two shields. G・G・GWhile your hand is down to one card or fewer, your other creatures all gain Speed Attacker. On attackWhenever one of your creatures attacks, flip the top card of your deck. If it is a Beat Jockey or a Dragon costing no more than the attacker, you may put it straight into play; otherwise it goes to the bottom.

This is the engine card. Handing an entire board Speed Attacker turns every creature you deploy that turn into an immediate threat, and the attack trigger keeps deploying more — each attack is another look at the deck.

Flow "GIG" Roll

Flow "GIG" Roll

《フロウ“GIG”ロール》 · GIG is read gig · Uncommon
Type NEO Creature Race Beat Jockey Cost 7 Power 2000+
NEO EvolutionStacks onto a Fire creature, or is played as an ordinary creature. On summonYou may put one of your own shields into the graveyard to reduce its cost by five. ConditionalIf any player is down to two shields or fewer, it gains +4000 power and Double Breaker.

A seven-cost card that becomes a two-cost card if you are willing to take damage for it. And the second ability means the shield you spent makes the creature bigger — the cost and the payoff are the same action.

Note that the condition reads any player. Your opponent breaking your shields switches it on just as well as you doing it yourself.

Sukebo Churis

Sukebo Churis

《スケボー・チュリス》 · Common
Type Creature Race Beat Jockey Cost 3 Power 2000
On playChoose one: discard a card and draw two, or destroy an opposing creature of power 2000 or less.

The modest one, and the one that ties the set together. Discarding to draw is how a Fire deck reaches an empty hand on purpose — and an empty hand is what the other cards are waiting for.

And the one Corocoro got

A Fire spell revealed separately, earlier in the day, with a keyword that is a stricter version of the same idea: free if it is the only card in your hand, then a board wipe. Its own page is here.

Four cards, one instruction

Empty your hand. Spend your shields. The cards that reward you for having nothing are the cards this set is built around. What the first four reveals have in common

This is not new ground for the game — the most-played deck in the format already runs a card with G・G・G, and Fire decks empty their hands as a matter of course. What is new is how much of one set is pointed at it.

Worth holding lightly, though. Four cards is four cards, and a set has far more than that. The Baraghiara reprints at the centre of it are Nature, and they are doing something else entirely.
