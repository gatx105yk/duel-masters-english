PLAY'S · EVENT · 21 OCT 2026

The seventh collaboration opens on 22 October, and took seven days to be documented

The seventh にじさんじ collaboration was announced in a live broadcast on 12 October. The official notice went up on 19 October — seven days later, three days before it opens. Until then the date, the cards, the skins and the theme song existed only as posts on X. This is what the notice says, what the posts said before it, and which parts are still undocumented the day before launch.

What this page covers, and when Written on 21 October from the official notice published 19 October, the maintenance notice published the same day, and the publisher's X posts from 12 to 19 October. The collaboration opens the following day and nothing here has been seen in the running game.

Three further notices are promised in the one that exists — for the set, the deck builders and the Legend Battle — and none of them had appeared by 18:00 on 20 October. Card contents, prices and the Legend Battle's reward tiers are therefore not known.

All four from the official notice. The Legend Battle ends on 25 November at 23:59 and the shop closes at the maintenance on the 26th, so about ten hours apart rather than the full day the dates suggest.

What is in the shop

Four things go on sale: a collaboration set, the deck builders, a playmat and a protect. Two of them have published contents.

The コラボセット is five DMPP-37 packs, one collaboration prize card of SR or above, and one skin chip. DMPP-37 is the current set, so this is the live pack rather than an older one.

The deck builders are 36 cards each and there are five of them, each carrying one type of collaboration prize card. The notice gives the count as five and does not tie them to particular livers; the daily card-list posts do, one per liver. The publisher described them on 12 October as suited to beginners and as carrying prize cards that go well with the collaboration set. It did not say how, and the notice does not either.

The prize cards do not add up, and nothing explains the difference Ten prize cards were named on X, two for each liver. The notice accounts for six: one type in each of the five deck builders, and one card of SR or above in the collaboration set.

Where the other four sit is not stated anywhere. They might be the pool the set draws its one card from, or they might come from the Legend Battle, or from somewhere not yet announced. This page is not going to pick one. The three promised notices are the place that would answer it, and they do not exist yet.

Prices are not published. Neither is the card list of any deck builder beyond the images posted daily on X, which are too small to read.

Codes are read off the product box in the card-list images, which are 680 pixels wide. Two of the five do not resolve at that size and are not guessed here. The codes do not run in reveal order. For comparison, the Railgun collaboration in August ran DMPC-11 to DMPC-13 across three products.

Two differences in how the five were handled are worth recording because they are visible in the posts rather than stated anywhere. リゼ・ヘルエスタ's skin is a 皇族衣装 variant rather than her default look. And 栞葉るり, the last of the five, had both her prize cards announced in a single post where the other four got one post each — her card-list post also carries the line 「※商品パッケージに関して、一部変更がございました」, a change to the product packaging, with no further detail.

Two of these cards are also the names of ranked seasons 《オラマッハ・ザ・ジョニー》, revealed on 16 October, is the name of the ranked season that started that same day and runs to 15 November. 《超神星HADES・ドラゲリオン》, revealed on 17 October, is the name of the season that had just ended, and is also what players call a deck.

This matters for anyone searching Japanese posts. A search on either name returns posts about the season, the deck and now a collaboration card, with nothing in the text to separate them. This site has already been caught by that once, on the Dragerion name, and had to restructure a page around it.

The Legend Battle

All five livers appear in it. Legend Points pay out special-illustration cards, protects and titles, and the story mode is given a collaboration treatment. It runs from the 22nd to 25 November 23:59.

Reward tiers are not published. The notice points at a further notice that does not exist yet. The current Legend Battle, 「深淵より時を歪めて」, ends at 23:59 on 21 October, and the new one starts after a maintenance that runs to 15:00 on the 22nd — so there is a gap of about fifteen hours between them, not a clean handover.

Seven days of nothing

The collaboration was announced on 12 October during a live broadcast, in nine posts over sixteen minutes. The first page on the publisher's news index appeared on 19 October.

That gap is unusual for this publisher rather than normal. The Railgun collaboration was revealed on 23 August and documented on 25 August, two days later, and ended up with four notices. This one ran a week on X alone, and in that week the publisher posted the opening date, five skins, ten prize cards, a theme song with full credits, and the fact that the sixth collaboration's skins and cards return — none of it on a page that could be cited.

It is worth being plain about what that does and does not mean. It is a documentation lag, not a sign of anything wrong with the collaboration. The streamer event that ran on 12 October — the same broadcast this collaboration was announced in — still has no page on the news index eight days later, so a documented event is not guaranteed at all. What it changes is what a reader outside Japan could find: for seven days, the answer was nothing, unless they were watching the X account.

What is still not known the day before

  • Three promised notices — the set, the deck builders and the Legend Battle. None had appeared by 18:00 on 20 October.
  • Prices for any of the four shop items.
  • Card lists for the deck builders, beyond images too small to read.
  • Legend Battle reward tiers.
  • What the packaging change was, noted on 19 October without detail.
  • Two client features announced on 12 October as due this month: 『デュエプレ・レッスン』, a mode that teaches a topic through play, and 『ランダムスキン機能』, which picks a favourited skin per match. The Ver.4.8.1 maintenance notice for the 22nd does not list either of them in its published contents.

The same maintenance changes the Quick Pick card pool from eleven products to fourteen, adding DMPP-33 through 35 mid-season.
