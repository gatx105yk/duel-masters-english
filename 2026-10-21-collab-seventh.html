PLAY'S · EVENT · 21 OCT 2026

The seventh collaboration opens on 22 October, and took seven days to be documented

The seventh にじさんじ collaboration was announced in a live broadcast on 12 October. The official notice went up on 19 October — seven days later, three days before it opens. Until then the date, the cards, the skins and the theme song existed only as posts on X. This is what the notices say, what the posts said before them, and which parts are still undocumented the day before launch.

Update, 02:00 on 22 October — the opening time is no longer fixed An emergency maintenance began at 23:15 on 21 October, announced four minutes before it started, with no end time given. The publisher states its purpose as system security measures, and says nothing else about it.

The same notice adds that the 10:00–15:00 update maintenance planned for the 22nd may be run on continuously from the emergency one, depending on the situation. That is the publisher's own wording and the only thing it has said about the timing. Everything below describes what was announced before this.

A second notice at 00:43 on the 22nd says the website is under emergency maintenance for the same stated reason, end time to be announced separately. At 02:00 the news index was unreachable, redirecting to a Takara Tomy group maintenance page, so none of this exists on a page that can be cited — it is on X only.
Corrected 21 October This page was wrong when it was published. It said the three promised notices — for the Collab Set, the deck builders and the Legend Battle — “had not appeared by 18:00 on 20 October”. All three were published at 17:00 on 20 October, about an hour before this page went up. The last check made here was at 16:56, and the sentence then asserted a negative for a time that had not been checked.

What that error carried with it, all corrected below: prices were said to be unpublished, and are published; the deck builders were said not to be tied to particular livers, and are; the two product codes given as “not legible” are published as text, and one code this page did print was wrong — it gave DMPC-18 to 鈴原るる, whose code is DMPC-16; DMPC-18 is 栞葉るり. The box headed “the prize cards do not add up” has been replaced with the answer.

The headline claim is unchanged. The first notice still appeared on 19 October, seven days after the reveal.
What this page covers, and when Written on 21 October from the collaboration notice of 19 October, the maintenance notice of the same day, the three product notices and the card-ability notice of 20 October, and the publisher's X posts from 12 to 20 October. The collaboration opens the following day and nothing here has been seen in the running game.

What is still missing is now narrow: the Legend Battle's point thresholds, and two places where the publisher's own sources contradict each other. Both are marked below.

What is in the shop

The event is 「仮想の決闘者 第7弾」 — roughly Virtual Duelist, seventh, translated here rather than official — and the deck-builder line carries the same name. The official pages write it with 第7弾; the daily card-list posts on X write 『コラボデッキビルダー 仮想の決闘者』 without it.

The 19 October notice said four things go on sale: the Collab Set, the deck builders, a playmat and a protect. The 20 October deck-builder notice describes the playmat and the protect differently — not as items on sale, but as a first-purchase bonus given with each deck builder, carrying the same illustration as that deck's premium card. The two official notices are not saying the same thing, and this page is not picking one.

The Collab Set — コラボセット, the name the August collaboration used too — is 1700 gems for five DMPP-37 packs, one collaboration prize card and one skin chip, and can be bought up to twenty times. DMPP-37 is the current set, so this is the live pack rather than an older one. The 19 October notice called the prize card SR以上, SR or above; the 20 October notice does not repeat that, and instead names the pool it is drawn from.

That card is one of five, at random, and cannot be chosen. All five are premium-only — full foil, with part of the card's background animated. Four skin chips exchange for one of the five collaboration skins. The 20 October notice also gives the collaboration a fuller title than any earlier source, 『仮想の決闘者 第7弾 -ROYALURULER UNDER FARM-』, and says the sixth collaboration's set returns in a 2026 version alongside it.

The deck builders are 36 cards each and there are five of them, 2000 gems each, one purchase apiece. The 20 October notice ties each to a named liver and a product code, which the 19 October notice did not. Each carries one collaboration card in premium form, four copies of it. Because each set is under forty cards, the notice states plainly that none of them can be played as a deck on its own. The publisher described them on 12 October as suited to beginners and as carrying prize cards that go well with the Collab Set. It did not say how, and the notices still do not.

The prize cards now add up This page said on 20 October that ten prize cards had been named on X, that the notice then available accounted for six, and that where the other four sat was not stated anywhere. It gave three possibilities and declined to pick one.

The 20 October notices answer it, and the first of the three was the right one. The ten split evenly. The five cards revealed first — one per liver — are the pool the Collab Set draws its single random card from; the five revealed second are the premium cards in the five deck builders, four copies each. Nothing among the ten comes from the Legend Battle: the card it pays out, 《D.A.P.I. ROCK》, is an eleventh and had not been announced at all.

Prices and full contents for all five deck builders are now published as text, which the daily card-list images on X were too small to give. The contents themselves are not reproduced here.

This page first read them off the product box in the card-list images, which are 680 pixels wide: two would not resolve at that size, and one that did was read wrong — DMPC-18 was given to 鈴原るる and belongs to 栞葉るり. † The X post of 19 October names 《守護水龍 ハイドランジア》 as one of 栞葉るり's two cards, in an image captioned to say both are obtainable by buying the Collab Set. That card appears in none of the 20 October notices. The deck builder names 《涼夏妖精カクラン》 instead, and the Collab Set's five-card pool does not include it either. For the other four livers the X posts and the notices agree exactly. The publisher's two sources disagree on this one card; this page is not resolving it, and leaves the announced name in the table.

Two differences in how the five were handled are worth recording because they are visible in the posts rather than stated anywhere. リゼ・ヘルエスタ's skin is a 皇族衣装 variant rather than her default look. And 栞葉るり, the last of the five, had both her prize cards announced in a single post where the other four got one post each — her card-list post also carries the line 「※商品パッケージに関して、一部変更がございました。」, a change to the product packaging, with no further detail.

The publisher has since corrected two of its own images. The deck-builder notice of 20 October opens with an apology for the 「配信者王決定戦」 broadcast of 12 October, in which the box front shown for DMPC-18 was wrong; that notice carries the corrected art. Separately, an X post at 21:30 on 20 October corrected the hat on 鈴原るる's skin in an earlier image. Whether either is the undetailed packaging change noted on 19 October is not stated anywhere, and the three are not treated here as the same thing.

Two of these cards are also the names of ranked seasons 《オラマッハ・ザ・ジョニー》, revealed on 16 October, is the name of the ranked season that started that same day and runs to 15 November. 《超神星HADES・ドラゲリオン》, revealed on 17 October, is the name of the season that had just ended, and is also what players call a deck.

This matters for anyone searching Japanese posts. A search on either name returns posts about the season, the deck and now a collaboration card, with nothing in the text to separate them. This site has already been caught by that once, on the Dragerion name, and had to restructure a page around it.

The Legend Battle

All five livers appear in it. Legend Points pay out special-illustration cards, protects and titles, and the story mode is given a collaboration treatment. It runs from 22 October to 25 November 23:59.

The 20 October notice names what the points buy: a special-illustration 《D.A.P.I. ROCK》 prize card, DMPP-37 SR tickets, a collaboration protect and a title, with a further title for clearing the 伝説級 difficulty. It still gives no point thresholds, so how much play any of it takes is unknown. Prize cards here cannot be disassembled and cannot take a premium ticket. The current Legend Battle, 「深淵より時を歪めて」, ends at 23:59 on 21 October, and the new one starts after a maintenance that runs to 15:00 on the 22nd — so there is a gap of about fifteen hours between them, not a clean handover.

Seven days of nothing

The collaboration was announced on 12 October during a live broadcast, in nine posts over sixteen minutes. The first page on the publisher's news index appeared on 19 October.

How long documentation takes here varies a great deal, and this sits at the slow end of it. The Railgun collaboration was revealed on 23 August and documented on 25 August, two days later, and ended up with four notices. This one ran a week on X alone, and in that week the publisher posted the opening date, five skins, ten prize cards, a theme song with full credits, and the fact that the sixth collaboration's skins and cards return — none of it on a page that could be cited.

The streamer event that ran on 12 October — the same broadcast this collaboration was announced in — still has no page at all, nine days later. Two days, seven days, never: a documented event is not something to count on.

It is worth being plain about what this does and does not mean. It is a documentation lag, not a sign of anything wrong with the collaboration. What it changes is what a reader outside Japan could find: for seven days, the answer was nothing, unless they were watching the X account.

What is still not known the day before

  • Legend Battle point thresholds. The rewards are named; what it costs to reach any of them is not.
  • Which card is 栞葉るり's second — X and the product notice name different cards, and nothing reconciles them.
  • Whether the playmat and protect are sold or given — the two notices describe them differently.
  • What the packaging change was, noted on 19 October without detail, and whether the two images corrected since are it.
  • Two client features announced on 12 October as due this month: 『デュエプレ・レッスン』, a mode that teaches a topic through play, and 『ランダムスキン機能』, which picks a favourited skin per match. The Ver.4.8.1 maintenance notice for the 22nd does not list either of them in its published contents.

The same maintenance changes the Quick Pick card pool from eleven products to fourteen, adding DMPP-33 through 35 mid-season.

It also changes two cards. 《“B-零朱”レイド》 and 《火夜のフラム・アルテ》 are both altered at the 22 October maintenance, announced at 17:00 on 20 October alongside the three collaboration notices. That is a separate story from this one and is covered on its own page.
