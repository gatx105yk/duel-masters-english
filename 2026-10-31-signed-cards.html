The card everyone got, and the result nobody posted

The seventh collaboration’s five signed prize cards went into 「カード交換」 late on 30 October, and one of them is being given to every player free. It is 鈴原るる’s.

Twenty-three minutes before that giveaway post, mid-stream, the official account had asked which of two livers would win and said the winner’s signed card would be the prize. It has posted no result, and it has not said the free card is that prize. Both posts are below; the join between them is not.

Corrected 31 October, within the hour of publishing This page went up headed “Everyone was given the winner’s card, and nobody posted who won”, and its opening said the free card was decided by the duel. Nothing published says that. The account announced a prize rule during the stream and announced a giveaway twenty-three minutes later, and joining the two is exactly the inference this site’s own rules forbid. The body always said so; the headline did not, which is the worse way round.

news/16475, 「【ショップ】サイン付きカードがプライズカード交換に登場！」. The signed collaboration prize cards of all five livers go into the prize-card exchange.

The notice carries four 注意事項. Three of them are easy to miss, and the fourth is the usual reservation that the period and contents may change without notice. A premium ticket cannot be used on a prize card — the 8,000 points is the only route to the premium version. Prize cards cannot be dismantled or crafted. And the notice says a limited-run prize card may be offered again later, so it does not describe this as a last chance.

Twenty-three minutes separate the question from the prize. The publisher has not posted a result, on the official account or on the news index, and the giveaway post does not mention the duel at all. The two posts are reported here as two posts.

Nor is the free copy documented anywhere but X. news/16475 covers the exchange and says nothing about a card being given to every player; that is in the 22:39 post and nowhere else, checked 31 October.

Corrected 31 October, within the hour of publishing. This paragraph first grouped the 《ヘブンズ・ゲート》 revival with the scenarios and said “neither has a news-index page”. The revival does — it is the second half of news/16475, which this page says two sections above. The sentence contradicted the article it was in.

And so did the other half. The same paragraph then said the returning scenarios had no news-index page. They have one, news/16426, which has been on the index since 22 October. It was missed because this site believed the index held only ten items — it does not; /news/page/2 and onward work, and the notice is there. The ?pg=2 form that was tested returns the first page, which is where that belief came from.

栞葉るり’s signed card is 《ハラグロ祭り～アイドルのその先へ～》. A card whose name begins the same way appears in her deck builder’s card list, published as an image on 29 October; that list truncates the name, so the match is between an image read and this post, not between two text sources. The record of the two cards announced for her is on the collaboration page; this is one more published card of hers and it is not the disputed one.

The notice came at 23:00

Twenty-two October notices have now had their timestamps read. Ten carry 5:00 PM. Twelve do not, and nine of those twelve are one day: everything that went up for the collaboration’s opening on 22 October is timestamped 4:00 PM. The rest are news/16522 at 6:00 PM on the 23rd, news/16308 at 12:00 AM on the 16th, and this one at 11:00 PM, eighteen minutes after the last of the posts that followed the stream.

So there is no single daily slot, and a notice attached to something — a launch, a broadcast — follows that thing rather than a clock. A check of the news index that evening at any time before 23:00 would not have found this notice; the X posts were up from 22:16. This site has put a false claim up once by checking four minutes early, which is why the gap is worth stating rather than glossing.

Corrected 31 October, within the hour of publishing This section first said “Twelve October notices have had their timestamps read here. Ten carry 5:00 PM… Two do not”, and framed 23:00 as the outlier against a 17:00 slot. The sample was the problem. Nine more October notices were a click away on the second page of the news index, all of them at 4:00 PM, and a tenth at midnight. The tally was true of what had been read and false about the publisher, which is the failure mode this site keeps writing rules about. It was found by a fact-check pass that went looking for the sample rather than the arithmetic.
