It's Railgun

The collaboration teased for 20:00 on 23 August is A Certain Scientific Railgun T 『とある科学の超電磁砲T』. Misaka Mikoto and company are coming to Duel Masters City.

It is live now, and runs into late September. Five cards, three prebuilt character decks, a Legend Battle and a dedicated room-match mode. The reaction split early — the pairings are loved, the card choices are not — and the deck builder announced afterwards reads like a reply to that.

What was actually said

The full contents of the announcement TV anime A Certain Scientific Railgun T collaboration confirmed. Misaka Mikoto and the others are coming to Duel Masters City. Tags: #デュエプレ #超電磁砲T #とある. Posted 23 August 2026, 20:00 JST.

No release date has been given. No cards, no skins, no campaign structure. The teaser that preceded it — posted three days earlier with no content at all — drew 510,000 views; the reveal itself is running ahead of that pace.

Twenty-two hours in those figures are 3.31 million, 10,000 and 4,664 — the view count multiplied by more than ten while likes doubled and reposts grew by half. For scale: a routine card reveal from the same account draws 25,000 to 35,000 views. This is roughly a hundred times that, for a post containing one sentence.

What Railgun is, if you have not met it

A spin-off of the light novel series A Certain Magical Index, following Misaka Mikoto — a middle-school student in a city of engineered psychics, and one of only seven ranked at the highest tier of ability. Her power is electricity, and the title refers to her signature move: firing a coin at three times the speed of sound.

Railgun T is the third season, broadcast in 2020. The franchise has been running since 2004 and remains large enough to sustain a constant stream of merchandise, pop-up stores and collaborations — which is context worth having for why this announcement moved the numbers it did.

Delight, mostly Posts along the lines of: genuinely love Railgun, was exhausted from work and this wiped it out. Several people reporting they had read the manga recently and are now looking forward to it. Kuroko's popularity is doing visible work here.
Surprise at the pairing A recurring note of bafflement rather than objection — that this came out of nowhere. One frequently repeated observation: Railgun collaborates with everything, but never with Index, despite Index being the parent series with the actual protagonist.
People who guessed wrong At least one detailed prediction circulating for a different franchise entirely, with proposed card-to-character mappings already worked out. That poster's follow-up thought — that the cards they had in mind could not compete in the current format anyway — is a small window into how the metagame conversation colours everything.
Immediately practical Within twenty minutes, someone had posted a costing guide for newcomers: how the skin-chip exchange works, four chips per skin, and an estimate of roughly 6,000 yen to get one, gems being priced at about one yen each. The community's reflex is to work out the price before the product exists.
Speculation about voice lines A running joke about which of Kuroko's catchphrases will replace the standard shield-break call. Not information, but a decent illustration of how much of a PLAY'S collaboration is expected to be cosmetic — skins, voice lines and cut-ins rather than mechanics.
Live now Running since 27 August, until 24 September. The Legend Battle story has finished releasing — five episodes plus an ending. All four skins are out: Misaki Shokuhou (Kyomi Asakura), Kazari Uiharu (Aki Toyosaki), Kuroko Shirai (Satomi Arai) and Mikoto Misaka (Rina Satou).

A second event opened on 3 September: the Tower of Trials, a consecutive-battle mode with the Railgun cast as bosses, running to 23 September. It carries a seventh collaboration card — and, notably, rental decks.

The cards

Eight so far, all PR rarity. Five different acquisition routes, and the route is printed on each card's promotional art: most require buying a deck builder, one comes from the Collab Set, and two are earnable — one through the Legend Battle, one through the Tower of Trials — and one arrives simply for logging in, from a separate Toaru tie-in running at the same time.

Tensei no Seisai Z · cost 3 spellLegend Battle 《転生ノ正裁Z》 · 御坂美琴

S-Back. You may put one of your shields into your hand, but its Shield Trigger cannot be used. When cast from hand, instead of going to the graveyard it goes face-up on top of one of your shields.

Damma Vogaiga · cost 4, power 4000Deck builder 《堕魔 ヴォガイガ》 · 食蜂操祇

On entering the battle zone, mills the top four cards of your deck and returns one Darkness card from your graveyard to hand. Reduces the summon cost of your Madougu creatures by one, to a minimum of one.

Sounandesu · cost 6, power 7000Deck builder 《ソーナンデス》 · 佐天涙子＆初春飾利

Jokers race. Mach Fighter, J-Change 8, W-Breaker. When it leaves the battle zone you may discard a card, and if you do, return a creature from your mana zone to hand.

Tamashii Ugatsu Kousei no Seisai Z · cost 7 spellDeck builder 《魂穿ツ煌世ノ正裁Z》 · 御坂美琴＆白井黒子

S-Back. Takes an opponent's non-evolution creature of cost 8 or less and puts it face-up on top of one of their shields. Like its cost-3 counterpart, casting it from hand puts it on your own shield rather than in the graveyard.

Manji Moon Ga Ryuzaku Manji / Manji・Goku・SatsuDeck builder 《卐月 ガ・リュザーク 卐／卐・獄・殺》 · 食蜂操祇

The first card revealed, and the finisher the Shokuhou deck is built toward. Full text has not been shown.

Don Jungle S7 · cost 8, power 8000+Collab Set 《ドンジャングルS7》 · 初春飾利

Nature. Mach Fighter, W-Breaker. On entering the battle zone, you may put a creature of power 7000 or less from your mana zone into the battle zone. Gains +6000 power during battle, and forces opposing creatures to attack it where possible — a taunt that wins most fights it is dragged into.

Damma DrinriTower of Trials 《堕魔 ドゥリンリ》

Special-art PR, earned with points from the Tower of Trials event rather than bought. Full text has not been published.

Brain Touch, premiumLogin only 《ブレイン・タッチ》

Not from the Railgun collaboration. This one marks a different Toaru series — the anime A Certain Unauthorised Girls' Cohabitation, airing from 9 October — and is given away for logging in, alongside a matching sleeve. Runs on the same dates as everything else.

Two of these are the same card at different sizes Tensei no Seisai Z and Tamashii Ugatsu Kousei no Seisai Z share a design: both are S-Back spells that, when cast from hand, sit face-up on your own shield instead of going to the graveyard — so they can be used twice. The cost-3 version draws you a shield; the cost-7 version buries an opposing creature under one of theirs.

This is the deck the complaints are about The most-shared criticism of the launch is that new players are picking up Savvark to play Misaka and getting run over by Raid. This is that deck. Note the curve: nothing before cost 2, and three of its four top-end slots sit at cost 7. Against a format where decks kill on turn four, a deck that wants to reach seven mana is a difficult first purchase.
And a partial answer to them The Tower of Trials, which opened 3 September, supplies rental decks — the official notice says you can play immediately without building anything. That is the same mechanism Battle Arena uses, applied to the event most likely to be a new player's second stop after the Legend Battle. Whether it was a response to the launch complaints or was always planned, it addresses the same problem: someone who arrived for the anime does not have a deck.

Note the curve: this one starts at cost 1 and tops out at 8, against Shokuhou's 2-to-8. It is the faster of the two.

This is worth noting against the "it's a misreading of the character" objection: Shokuhou is not being given a Deszark deck as such. She is being given Madougu, which builds into Ga Ryuzaku as its endpoint. Whether that fits a character whose power is mental control is still an aesthetic judgement, but the mechanical identity is graveyard-building, not Deszark.

The deck builder is the answer to the complaint Alongside the cards, PLAY'S is shipping A Certain Scientific Duelist — prebuilt decks sold by character, with published card lists, explicitly aimed at newcomers. Three have been detailed: Misaka and Kuroko, Shokuhou, and Saten with Uiharu. The post announcing it has 780,000 views — more than any individual card reveal. If the objection was that collaboration cards arrive with nothing to put them in, this is the counter-offer.

They are purchases, not gifts. The card art itself carries the line "obtainable by buying the Collab Deck Builder". Three products: DMPC-11 Misaka and Kuroko, DMPC-12 Uiharu and Saten, DMPC-13 Shokuhou.

The official website finally has pages for this. As of 25 August the Legend Battle has a full notice with reward tiers, collaboration sleeves and titles listed. It went up two days after the reveal.

The argument that broke out

Reaction to the reveal itself was near-uniform delight. Reaction to the cards was not. Within hours of the first card appearing, the conversation had split into two arguments running at once.

The pairing landed Shokuhou with Ga Ryuzaku is drawing a lot of approval — posts calling the match perfect, and people reporting the announcement hit them harder than expected. Not everyone agrees: at least one widely-shared post objects that casting her as a Dark-civilization Deszark deck is a misreading of the character.
The cards are not competitive The substantive complaint. Paraphrasing the most-shared version: the collaboration is genuinely good, but apart from Shokuhou's card everything is outside the current format — so you can pay for collaboration cards and then not be able to use them on the ranked ladder. The word being used is sad rather than angry.

This complaint predates the deck builder announcement. Whether prebuilt character decks answer it depends on what you wanted the cards for — they solve "I have nothing to put this in", not "this is not ladder-viable".
Expecting a balance patch to follow A related read, and one that appeared in English too: most of the chosen cards are past their prime, so a balance adjustment shipping alongside the event would make sense. Nothing has been announced.
Cost, immediately Posts working out whether the skin alone is affordable, and one noting they want it but next month's money is already spoken for. The costing reflex from announcement day has not faded.
And the internet being the internet A non-trivial share of the discussion concerns whether Shokuhou's skin art shows her underwear. The prevailing conclusion is that it is the lining of her skirt. This is reported for completeness rather than because it is informative.
What is confirmed and what is not The date, the first card and the first skin come from the official account. The claim that the other cards are uncompetitive is a player assessment, not a statement about card text — no full card list has been published, and no balance changes have been announced.

What to expect, based on how PLAY'S usually does this

Previous PLAY'S collaborations have run to skins, voiced dialogue, alternate-art cards and themed campaigns rather than new mechanics. A third round with the card game WIXOSS concluded in July.

None of that is confirmed for this one. The announcement contains a franchise name and nothing else. Anything about dates, contents or format is guesswork until Takara Tomy publishes more.

Also still unannounced: the paper set built from PLAY'S cards, which is confirmed for 7 November but has had only two cards revealed.
