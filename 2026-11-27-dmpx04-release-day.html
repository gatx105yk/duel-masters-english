PLAY’S · News · 27 Nov 2026

The pack is out, and the two markers finally mean something

DMPX-04 went live at 4:00 PM JST on 26 November, at the end of the maintenance. The release notice, news/16648, does two things this site had been waiting on: it expands （Sec） and （PCC）, and it names which cards carry them.

What it does not do is say how many cards are in the pack.

The markers

一部のカードは特別なシークレットバージョンが出現する場合があります！

（Sec） is a シークレットバージョン — a secret version that some cards may turn up as. Four cards have one, and each carries a 初回特典, a first-time bonus:

CardFirst-time bonus
《神聖岐 スターゲイザー/スペクトラム・サイン》 skin カノン（新星）, a protect, a playmat, and the BGM 『Dual Place』 for some duels
《「祝誓」の頂 ウェディング・グレイス》 skin ウェディング（新星）, a protect, a playmat, and the same BGM
《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー/インビンシブル・テクノロジー》 a protect and a playmat
《暗黒破壊皇インフェルノ・シーザー/超煉獄魔弾 DOOM》 a protect and a playmat
一部のカードは、デュエプレオリジナルキャラクターのイラストを使用したPLAY'S CHARACTER CARDバージョン(PCC)で封入されています。

（PCC） is a PLAY'S CHARACTER CARDバージョン, printed with original PLAY’S character art rather than the card’s usual illustration. Two cards are in the pack that way: 《ウェディング・ゲート》 and 《レレディ・バ・グーバ/ツインパクト・マップ》. No bonus is listed for either; they are a printing, not a reward.

Four and two — the same four and the same two the card list’s own ids had already separated out.

The reveal page worked those groups out on the morning of the 26th, before this notice existed, from nothing but the card list’s entry ids: four card numbers carried a second entry, and two more ended in a different suffix and rendered with a character-card badge. That page said at the time that it was recording a correspondence and not a definition. The definition arrived at 4:00 PM, about six and a half hours after that page went up, and did not move a single card between the groups.

Every one of the ten is in the thirty-eight the account posted, checked name against the reveal table rather than read off it. So the publisher’s own shortlist is drawn entirely from the cards it had already shown, and none of the pack’s unposted cards is promoted here.

Still no card count

The notice gives no total. Every figure in it was pulled out and classified rather than scanned for — thirty-eight numbers, and all of them are dates, the set code DMPX-04, gem prices, ticket counts, selection points or campaign figures. Not one is a count of cards in the pack, and no phrase of the 収録…種 shape appears at all. So the count on this site is still the one taken off the card list: 96 entries resolving to 92 distinct cards, which the reveal page shows the working for. That figure was read again at 10:11 JST on 27 November, after release, and had not moved.

A selection ticket is exchanged for a card of your choosing at the カード交換所 on the pack-buying screen. Which cards it covers is described only as 対象のカード — eligible cards — and the notice does not list them.

Logging in during the period gives one DMPX-04 SR ticket, which draws a random SR from the pack, and five days of logins give up to fifteen pack tickets. The period ends at the next regular card pack (次のレギュラーカードパックリリースまで), which has no date.

The two skins, and a song

Notice news/16650, posted the same hour, is about the two skins alone. Set either as your avatar and S・トリガー gets a cut-in of its own; set one as the home-screen navi and tapping it plays voice lines, with extra lines keyed to conditions the notice lists — a new pack, an event, a new notice, a present arriving.

The BGM that comes with the two Secret cards is 『Dual Place』, and the account gave its credits on X at 18:09: words by 星熊南巫, music and arrangement by void (Mournfinale), sung by カノン (CV 花澤香菜) and ウェディング (CV 大西沙織), produced by eMPIRE SOUND SYSTeMS. Those are the two characters the pack hands out as skins, and カノン and ウェディング are also two of the six names on the Legend Battle’s opponent list, which opened the same evening. The opponent list writes one of them differently — 無垢の守護者 カノン there against カノン（新星） here — and nothing published says the two are the same character in two versions, so this page does not say it either.

What else changed at 4:00 PM

Rental decks were rebuilt with the pack’s cards, so the new cards can be played without owning any. Quick Pick took them too, on top of the pool cut this site covered on the 24th. The Legend Battle 『黄金像とみんなの想い』 opened at 19:30 with its opening and first episode. And two campaigns went up at 21:00 and 21:01: up to 195 packs for a new account, and up to 160 for one that has been away three months or more.

The known-issues notice was updated the same afternoon and names none of the pack’s cards. news/10180, dated 26 November 4:00 PM, runs to twenty-one bullets across a fixed list and an outstanding one, and not one of them is a card from DMPX-04 — checked by searching the notice for each of the ten featured names and the markers’ six, at 10:16 JST on the 27th. That is a statement about what is published, one day in, and not about whether the cards work.

The account also renamed itself. Its display name was デュエル・マスターズ プレイス【公式】 through the whole reveal run and is now デュエル・マスターズ プレイス【デュエプレ公式】. None of the posts read here announces it — fifteen posts from 16:10 to 21:05 on the 26th — and this page does not know when it changed, only that the name was the old one in a harvest on the 25th and the new one in a harvest at 10:10 on the 27th. It is recorded because every harvest on this site quotes that name.

Corrected 27 November, within the hour of publishing Two things went out in the 10:18 deploy and were corrected in this one. This page said the two skin characters “are also two of the six opponents” in the Legend Battle. The opponent list writes one of them 無垢の守護者 カノン and the skin is カノン（新星）; the names match and nothing published says the two are one character in two versions, so the page now says what matches and stops. And it said “no post announces” the account’s rename, when fifteen posts from one evening were read — it now says that, and gives the two harvests the change sits between.

Also tightened: “there is no 全◯◯種 anywhere in it” guessed at a phrasing. Every one of the notice’s thirty-eight figures has now been listed and classified, which is a test rather than a guess, and the conclusion is unchanged.
