PLAY’S · News · 16 Nov 2026

The campaign had an answer, and paper already had the spell

A teaser went up on 13 November for a pack called 『PLAY’S TWINPACT PROJECT PACK』, and the pack now has a code: DMPX-04. Thirty-one cards have been revealed for it across seven nights, 14 to 20 November. This page began on 16 November with four of them and has carried a dated correction five times since, all of them still below; the count is as of 10:09 JST on 21 November.

The guessing campaign closed on 18 November at 23:59. On 19 November at 21:00 the card was revealed, and at 21:01 the answer was announced: the spell half is 《インビンシブル・テクノロジー》. One minute later the account posted something else about that spell, which has its own page here.

Both halves of the new twinpact exist in paper. The pairing does not.

Of the thirty-one: ten are already twinpacts in paper under the same full name; eight exist in paper as single-faced cards (six creatures, two spells); twelve are not in the paper database at all, on either half; and one has its spell half in paper and not its creature half. Ten, eight, twelve and one.

So the pack is not doing one thing. A third of it is paper twinpacts arriving in the digital game unchanged in name. A quarter is cards paper already has, getting a second face paper never gave them. And the largest group of all is names that are not in the paper catalogue at any width or spelling.

Six of the thirty-one were posted without a slash in the name — 《アカシック・ゼノン》, 《魔光大帝ネロ・グリフィスII世》, 《桜舞う門出》, 《アクア・レガトゥス》, 《ドンドン水撒くナウ》 and 《終末の監視者 ジ・ウォッチ》. What that means for those cards is not stated anywhere, and this page is not guessing.

And exactly one of the thirty-one was posted with spaces around the slash: 《氷牙レオポル・ディーネ公 / エマージェンシー・タイフーン》 on 20 November. The other twenty-four slashed names are written closed up. And the card database is not consistent either. Eight database titles were checked: seven put spaces around the slash, and 《阿修羅ンチュラ/傀儡が来る！》 — the card this site covered on 14 November — closes it up. So neither source has one rule, and a name copied from either may or may not match the other. This is recorded because it is the kind of difference that makes a search for one text fail to find the other, not because it means anything about the cards.

The 14 November seven came in a burst, not on the hour: one at 20:00, then six between 21:08 and 21:16. Six of those seven are 魔弾 or 魔光 cards, and the same evening the publisher ran a #デュエプレ研究所 premiere featuring a 『ツインパクト・ナイト』 deck built around 《暗黒破壊皇インフェルノ・シーザー/超煉獄魔弾 DOOM》. The 15th and 16th then went back to one card an hour from 18:00.

The most-viewed reveal of the thirty-one is the 17 November 《Dream idylle/究極・チャージャー》 at 785,697, which overtook the 14 November DOOM card at 748,312 at some point between the 17th and the 21st. Both figures are from the 10:09 pass on 21 November; the crossing itself was not watched and no date is claimed for it.

Every night from the 15th to the 20th ran four cards, one an hour from 18:00. Six nights, the same shape each time. Only the 14th was different. Whether the run continues past the 20th is not stated anywhere; nothing had been posted on 21 November as of 10:21 JST, which is early in the day by this run’s standards.

And the news index has published nothing about the pack at all. Thirty-one cards have been revealed on X; the index’s newest notice is from 16 November and concerns the ranked season. There is still no release date for DMPX-04 anywhere this page has looked.

The sixteenth card, and a claim this page could not support

《Dream idylle》 is not in the paper database, and because the name is Latin script that took four searches rather than one: half-width idylle returned 0, full-width ｉｄｙｌｌｅ returned 0, and the two controls both landed — NEX returned 114 and Dream returned 2, which are the two printings of 《God of Dream / Grenade of D-moll》. So the engine reads Latin, matches a Latin word inside a card name, and has no card with this one.

Its spell half does exist. 《究極・チャージャー》 is a paper spell — light, cost 3, with the チャージャー keyword — and it has exactly one printing, in DM26-EX5, which is the crossover set this site has been covering since August, the one that was the digital-only game printing itself onto cardboard.

What that does not establish is which game the card came from. It would be easy to write that the spell went PLAY’S to paper this month and is now coming back as half of a twinpact, and this page said something like that for about ten minutes. The paper database cannot support it. DM26-EX5 holds both PLAY’S cards marked with a P’S suffix and cards without one, and 《究極・チャージャー》 has no suffix — so it could be a PLAY’S card printed to paper without the marking, or a card made for the set. Nothing consulted here says which, and there is no PLAY’S card database to check against.

What is checkable is smaller and still worth having. The spell’s flavour text reads アカシック・タレスはいつでも究極の答えを求めている。 — and the flavour on the 14 November reveal 《アカシック・ゼノン》 reads 外の世界の情報を取り入れすぎたタレスを救うため、ゼノンも電脳世界へと旅立った。 The same character is named in both, one on a card printed in the crossover set and one on a card revealed for DMPX-04. That is a shared name in two flavour texts, which is what it is and no more.

One card’s name is spelled two ways, and a search will not bridge it

The reveal post writes 《魔光大帝ネロ・グリフィスII世》 with two Latin capital I characters, U+0049 twice. The paper database writes the same card 《魔光大帝ネロ・グリフィスⅡ世》 with one Roman-numeral character, U+2161.

The two were searched back to back, as card-name searches of strings identical in every other character, fifteen seconds apart: the publisher’s spelling returned カード検索 0枚. The database’s spelling returned 2 — DM23EX2 and DMEX17, both 種類 クリーチャー, cost 9, power 12000.

This is the same class of difference this site recorded for the capped card, where the notice and the card page disagreed on a single digit's width. It is worth writing down each time, because it is the difference between a search that finds the card and a search that reports it does not exist.

Corrected 16 November, two hours after publishing — this is the important one The section below said “DMPX-04 is the digital game doing something to a card that the paper game never did to it” as the direction of the pack, on the strength of the campaign card alone. It is the wrong way round for six of the sixteen cards, which were already twinpacts in paper before DMPX-04 existed — the table above gives each one. So the pack does two different things at once, and the page had seen only one of them: it brings paper twinpacts into the digital game, and it also makes twinpacts paper never had. The campaign card is in the second kind, and one consequence of that is checkable: the paper database cannot be used to look its spell half up, because there is no paper twinpact of it to look at. The headline holds for that card. It did not hold for the pack.

The campaign, and what it quietly tells you

A separate notice, news/16626, posted 14 November at 9:30 PM, opens a prediction campaign:

DMPX-04『PLAY’S TWINPACT PROJECT PACK』にて、《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー》がツインパクトになって登場！！

Players are asked to guess the name of the card on the spell half and post it with a hashtag:

キャンペーン期間中、既にデュエプレ内に実装されているカードの中から、そのツインパクトの呪文面のカード名を予想し、

Ten entrants are drawn for an SR ticket for the pack. A second draw of three is made from a narrower pool:

さらに、予想が的中した方の中から抽選で3名様に

From among those whose prediction was correct — four copies of 《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー/○○》, with the spell half left as ○○ in the publisher’s own prize line. The notes add that the match must be 完全一致, exact, and that the four copies are the 通常版 — the normal version, not one of the premium treatments.

The interesting part is the constraint on the answer:

・解答となる呪文は、2026年10月31日時点でデュエプレ内に実装されているカードです。

The spell is a card already in the digital game as of 31 October — so the twinpact is not a new spell attached to an old creature. It is two things that already exist, joined.

The campaign ran 2026年11月14日（土）21:00～ 2026年11月18日（水）23:59 and is closed; the hashtag was #ツヴァイランサーツインパクト計画 and up to five entries were allowed per person. The notice supplies its own worked example of an answer: エナジー・ライト. Among the entry conditions is ・日本国内にお住まいの方 — residence in Japan, alongside a public X account and a follow of @dmps_info. Those lines are why this page is describing the campaign rather than pointing readers at it.

The answer, announced one minute after the card

19 November, 21:00 JST. The reveal post, under the same tag as the other thirty:

《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー/インビンシブル・テクノロジー》
我が全力の槍、受け止められるかな！？――クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー

21:01. A second post, carrying the campaign hashtag:

【#ツヴァイランサーツインパクト計画 正解発表！】 正解は《インビンシブル・テクノロジー》！ たくさんのご投稿、ありがとうございました！

So the spell half was named on the card and confirmed as the answer sixty seconds apart, the day after entries closed.

Both halves exist in paper. This pairing does not.

A card-name search for インビンシブル・テクノロジー returns four results under two different names, and the split is the whole point:

Paper cardWhat it isPrintings
《インビンシブル・テクノロジー》呪文, water, VR, cost 132 — DM-06, DMX-16
《キング・シビレアシダケ / インビンシブル・テクノロジー》twinpact, nature, R, cost 3, power 20002 — DM22EX1, DM23BD6

The spell is already half of a twinpact in paper — with a different creature. In paper it is paired with 《キング・シビレアシダケ》. DMPX-04 pairs the same spell with 《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー》, whose own five paper printings carry no spell half at all.

So the new card is two cards that both exist in paper, joined in a way paper has not joined them. That is narrower than what this page claimed for five days and it is what the sources support. The creature side is set out below; the spell side is the table above.

What the paper database does not tell you is what the spell costs or does in the digital game. There are two paper cards called 《インビンシブル・テクノロジー》 — the standalone water spell at cost 13, and the spell side of the nature twinpact, whose own cost was not read for this page. Nothing published says which of them the digital version follows, or whether it follows either, and this page is not inferring it.

Five printings on paper, none of them a twinpact

Searching the official card database for ツヴァイランサー returns five results, and each one was opened. They are two distinct cards:

《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー》, from DM-14 転生編(ジェネレート・ギア) 第1弾 and reprinted in DMX-19 スーパーレア100%パック. A cost-8 evolution creature of 13000 power, リキッド・ピープル, with a T・ブレイカー and two lines above it:

進化-自分のリキッド・ピープル１体の上に置く。
このクリーチャーはブロックされない。

And 《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサーP’S》, the digital version printed onto paper this month in DM26-EX5 デュエマプレイス・コラボパック 「電脳対戦 -WAR OF PLAY’S-」, in three printings. Same cost, power and race; three of its lines:

G・ゼロ：自分のリキッド・ピープルが４つ以上あれば、このクリーチャーをコストを支払わずに召喚してもよい。
進化：リキッド・ピープル・クリーチャー１体の上に置く。
自分のリキッド・ピープルはブロックされない。

Two lines are not quoted above: ジャストダイバー, and one the database writes as {3T} T・ブレイカー — this page is not explaining that notation, because the database does not. Five lines in all, the same five on all three printings.

What matters here is what is absent from all five. Each page gives a single creature with a single block of abilities. None of the five has a spell half, so none of them is a twinpact, and the twinpact the campaign describes would be the first for this card.

DM26-EX5 was, as this site put it in August, the digital-only game printing itself onto cardboard. For this one card, DMPX-04 runs the other way: the digital game is giving it a form the paper game never printed. That is true of 《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー》 and it is not true of the pack — six of the sixteen revealed cards are paper twinpacts already, as the table above sets out. Both things are in the same pack.

This is not a claim that paper will follow. Nothing published says DMPX-04 cards are coming to paper, and the database shows no such card.

A detail for anyone comparing printings

The flavour text moved as the card did. DM-14:

あれから200年。旅路の果てに、双槍は究極の鋭さを手に入れた。

And the P’S printing in DM26-EX5:

あれから200年。双槍は完全なる究極の鋭さを手に入れた。

Same first sentence. After it, 旅路の果てに、 is gone and 完全なる has been put in front of 究極の鋭さ — the same two hundred years, arrived at differently.

Across the five printings there are four flavour texts and one blank. The DMX-19 reprint opens その二本の槍は、貫通の槍。 instead; the Secret printing numbered ㊙1 carries a line of dialogue, 私の戦略からは逃れられない！ — カイト; and the ㊙2超 printing has no flavour text at all, its flavour field running straight into the product listing. A search for one of these lines will not find the others, which is the practical reason this site records differences like them instead of treating them as one line.
