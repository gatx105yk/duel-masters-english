The digital game is giving a card a shape paper never did

A teaser went up on 13 November for a pack called 『PLAY’S TWINPACT PROJECT PACK』, and the pack now has a code: DMPX-04. Fifteen cards have been revealed for it — seven on 14 November, four on 15 November, four on 16 November — and this page was rewritten on 17 November because it had found only four of them.

The card the publisher has built a guessing game around is 《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー》. The name turns up five times in the official paper database — twice as itself, three times in its PLAY’S version — and not one of the five is a twinpact.

Six already twinpacts in paper under the same name. Four exist in paper as single-faced cards — three creatures and, in one case, a spell. Five are not in the paper database at all. The campaign card, 《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー》, is in the middle group, which is the subject of the rest of this page.

Three of the fifteen were posted without a slash in the name — 《アカシック・ゼノン》, 《魔光大帝ネロ・グリフィスII世》 and 《桜舞う門出》. What that means for those cards is not stated anywhere, and this page is not guessing.

The 14 November seven came in a burst, not on the hour: one at 20:00, then six between 21:08 and 21:16. Six of those seven are 魔弾 or 魔光 cards, and the same evening the publisher ran a #デュエプレ研究所 premiere featuring a 『ツインパクト・ナイト』 deck built around 《暗黒破壊皇インフェルノ・シーザー/超煉獄魔弾 DOOM》. The 15th and 16th then went back to one card an hour from 18:00.

No reveal had been posted on 17 November as of 17:44 JST, when this page was last checked against the account.

The count came from what was visible in the timeline without scrolling back, which is the third time in two days this site has counted what it could see instead of the whole series. One card’s name is spelled two ways, and a search will not bridge it

The reveal post writes 《魔光大帝ネロ・グリフィスII世》 with two Latin capital I characters, U+0049 twice. The paper database writes the same card 《魔光大帝ネロ・グリフィスⅡ世》 with one Roman-numeral character, U+2161.

The two were searched back to back, as card-name searches of strings identical in every other character, fifteen seconds apart: the publisher’s spelling returned カード検索 0枚. The database’s spelling returned 2 — DM23EX2 and DMEX17, both 種類 クリーチャー, cost 9, power 12000.

This is the same class of difference this site recorded for the capped card, where the notice and the card page disagreed on a single digit's width. It is worth writing down each time, because it is the difference between a search that finds the card and a search that reports it does not exist.

Corrected 16 November, two hours after publishing — this is the important one The section below said “DMPX-04 is the digital game doing something to a card that the paper game never did to it” as the direction of the pack, on the strength of the campaign card alone. It is the wrong way round for six of the fifteen cards, which were already twinpacts in paper before DMPX-04 existed — the table above gives each one. So the pack does two different things at once, and the page had seen only one of them: it brings paper twinpacts into the digital game, and it also makes twinpacts paper never had. The campaign card is in the second kind, and one consequence of that is checkable: the paper database cannot be used to look its spell half up, because there is no paper twinpact of it to look at. The headline holds for that card. It did not hold for the pack.

The names match the posts character for character, with one difference that belongs to the database and not the publisher: the card pages put spaces around the slash (ボルシャック・NEX / スーパー・スパーク) where the posts write it closed up and inside 《 》. And a control was run, because a dead search and a real zero look identical: ボルシャック as a card-name search returned 454 in the same session as every zero above.

The campaign, and what it quietly tells you

A separate notice, news/16626, posted 14 November at 9:30 PM, opens a prediction campaign:

DMPX-04『PLAY’S TWINPACT PROJECT PACK』にて、《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー》がツインパクトになって登場！！

Players are asked to guess the name of the card on the spell half and post it with a hashtag:

キャンペーン期間中、既にデュエプレ内に実装されているカードの中から、そのツインパクトの呪文面のカード名を予想し、

Ten entrants are drawn for an SR ticket for the pack. A second draw of three is made from a narrower pool:

さらに、予想が的中した方の中から抽選で3名様に

From among those whose prediction was correct — four copies of 《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー/○○》, with the spell half left as ○○ in the publisher’s own prize line. The notes add that the match must be 完全一致, exact, and that the four copies are the 通常版 — the normal version, not one of the premium treatments.

The interesting part is the constraint on the answer:

・解答となる呪文は、2026年10月31日時点でデュエプレ内に実装されているカードです。

The spell is a card already in the digital game as of 31 October — so the twinpact is not a new spell attached to an old creature. It is two things that already exist, joined.

The campaign runs 2026年11月14日（土）21:00～ 2026年11月18日（水）23:59, the hashtag is #ツヴァイランサーツインパクト計画, and up to five entries are allowed per person. The notice supplies its own worked example of an answer: エナジー・ライト. Among the entry conditions is ・日本国内にお住まいの方 — residence in Japan, alongside a public X account and a follow of @dmps_info. Those lines are why this page is describing the campaign rather than pointing readers at it.

Five printings on paper, none of them a twinpact

Searching the official card database for ツヴァイランサー returns five results, and each one was opened. They are two distinct cards:

《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー》, from DM-14 転生編(ジェネレート・ギア) 第1弾 and reprinted in DMX-19 スーパーレア100%パック. A cost-8 evolution creature of 13000 power, リキッド・ピープル, with a T・ブレイカー and two lines above it:

進化-自分のリキッド・ピープル１体の上に置く。
このクリーチャーはブロックされない。

And 《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサーP’S》, the digital version printed onto paper this month in DM26-EX5 デュエマプレイス・コラボパック 「電脳対戦 -WAR OF PLAY’S-」, in three printings. Same cost, power and race; three of its lines:

G・ゼロ：自分のリキッド・ピープルが４つ以上あれば、このクリーチャーをコストを支払わずに召喚してもよい。
進化：リキッド・ピープル・クリーチャー１体の上に置く。
自分のリキッド・ピープルはブロックされない。

Two lines are not quoted above: ジャストダイバー, and one the database writes as {3T} T・ブレイカー — this page is not explaining that notation, because the database does not. Five lines in all, the same five on all three printings.

What matters here is what is absent from all five. Each page gives a single creature with a single block of abilities. None of the five has a spell half, so none of them is a twinpact, and the twinpact the campaign describes would be the first for this card.

DM26-EX5 was, as this site put it in August, the digital-only game printing itself onto cardboard. For this one card, DMPX-04 runs the other way: the digital game is giving it a form the paper game never printed. That is true of 《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー》 and it is not true of the pack — six of the fifteen revealed cards are paper twinpacts already, as the table above sets out. Both things are in the same pack.

This is not a claim that paper will follow. Nothing published says DMPX-04 cards are coming to paper, and the database shows no such card.

A detail for anyone comparing printings

The flavour text moved as the card did. DM-14:

あれから200年。旅路の果てに、双槍は究極の鋭さを手に入れた。

And the P’S printing in DM26-EX5:

あれから200年。双槍は完全なる究極の鋭さを手に入れた。

Same first sentence. After it, 旅路の果てに、 is gone and 完全なる has been put in front of 究極の鋭さ — the same two hundred years, arrived at differently.

Across the five printings there are four flavour texts and one blank. The DMX-19 reprint opens その二本の槍は、貫通の槍。 instead; the Secret printing numbered ㊙1 carries a line of dialogue, 私の戦略からは逃れられない！ — カイト; and the ㊙2超 printing has no flavour text at all, its flavour field running straight into the product listing. A search for one of these lines will not find the others, which is the practical reason this site records differences like them instead of treating them as one line.
