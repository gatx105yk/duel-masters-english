The rulings for the pack came out before the pack

Fourteen entries went into the official card-rule FAQ before DMPX-04 existed — No.1013 to No.1026, and every one of them is about a card in the pack. The pack arrives in the maintenance running 10:00 to 16:00 JST today.

One card accounts for four of the fourteen.

Where the batch starts, and how that was established

The FAQ runs to 52 pages and its newest twenty are No.1007 to No.1026. Those twenty are not all about the pack: 1007 to 1012 are an earlier batch, turning on 《オラマッハ・ザ・ジョニー》 and 《卍 新世壊 卍》. From 1013 the subject changes and does not change back, and each of the fourteen from there names a card from the pack.

That boundary was found by opening each entry from 1007 upward and listing the card names it contains, not by reading down the page until it felt like the batch had started. The FAQ’s own card-set filter was tried first and does not apply — the result stays at 52 pages — so the count here is of entries read one by one.

《アカシック・ゼノン》 is in four of the fourteen — 1021, 1022, 1023 and 1026 — which is more than any other card here. All four turn on the same clause, an ability that works 「各ターンに一度」, once each turn.

Two of those four say the same thing, and it is the useful one

No.1021 — the ability triggered on one attack and was not used. Does it trigger again on a later attack the same turn? はい、またトリガーします。 Yes. 一度トリガーした際に使わなかったなら、「各ターンに一度」を使った扱いにはなりません。
No.1022 — the ability was used to cast a spell, but the spell was put into the graveyard by an opponent’s 《オラマッハ・ザ・ジョニー》 and never cast. Again? はい、またトリガーします。 Yes. 一度呪文を唱えようとしても、それが実際に唱えられなかったのであれば、「各ターンに一度」を使った扱いにはなりません。

So the once-per-turn is spent by the spell actually being cast, not by the trigger happening and not by the attempt. Two entries were published to say that, one for declining and one for being stopped.

Three more that are worth having in English

No.1026 — casting ≪スペルブック・チャージャー≫ through 《アカシック・ゼノン》: does the card go to the mana zone, as a Charger normally would? いいえ、マナゾーンに置かれません。 No. A Charger replaces the trip to the graveyard after the spell resolves; cast this way the card does not go to the graveyard, so there is nothing for the Charger to replace and 「チャージャー」能力は不発になります.
No.1020 — casting ≪魔弾バレット・バイス≫ and then ≪魔弾ソウル・キャッチャー≫ off one Meteorburn: can the second return the first? いいえ、できません。 No. The first card is already under the deck before the second is cast, so it is not in the graveyard to be returned.
No.1018 — four of the opponent’s creatures are replaced into the mana zone instead of being destroyed by 《「祝誓」の頂 ウェディング・グレイス》. Does the opponent discard four? いいえ、相手の手札は1枚も捨てさせません。 No — not one.

The translations above are this site’s and the Japanese is printed beside each answer, which is the only way a reader can check them. Where an answer runs longer in the original than it does here, the part reproduced is the reasoning the answer itself gives.

The FAQ writes a half and a whole card differently

Reading these fourteen turns up something the posts never showed. The FAQ uses two different bracket pairs, and it is not decoration:

FormWhat it marksExample
≪ ≫one face of a card, creature side or spell side≪魔弾バレット・バイス≫
《 》the whole card, both faces, slash and all《魔光ドラム・トレボール/魔弾バレット・バイス》

No.1020 uses both of those in one sentence, and so does 1025 (《炎龍宣言 鬼丸「爛武」/鬼丸秘伝ビクトリー・フレイム》 in hand, ≪鬼丸秘伝ビクトリー・フレイム≫ cast from it) and 1026 (≪氷牙アクア・マルガレーテ卿≫ attacking, 《氷牙アクア・マルガレーテ卿/スペルブック・チャージャー》 as the object that might move). Single-faced cards are always 《 》 — 《アカシック・ゼノン》, 《ヤッタレマン》, 《デーモン・ハンド》.

The convention held in every instance across the fourteen entries, which is how it is stated here: as a pattern that did not break, not as a rule the publisher has written down. Nothing in the FAQ explains the two forms, and this page has not found a notice that does.

It bears on something the reveal page has been careful about for ten days: the account posted eight of the thirty-eight names with no slash at all, and that page would not guess what that meant. This is not an answer to that — a ruling’s bracket is not a reveal post’s formatting. It does show the publisher has a working distinction between a face and a card, and uses it when precision matters.
