What is coming for the rest of 2026

Two games, two release schedules, one anniversary year. Duel Masters turns 25 and PLAY'S turns 7 in December. Here is everything dated, in English, in one place.

Dates marked est. are projections from established release patterns, not announcements. Everything else is confirmed.

How the two schedules work

Paper TCGPLAY'S
CadenceRoughly monthly, alternating set typesNew set every two months
Set codesDM26-RPn regular, EX extra, SD deck, BD deck, SP specialDMPP-nn regular, DMPX special, DMPB anniversary
Current arcGyakusatsu-hen （逆札篇） — "Ex Dream"Sōkyoku-hen （双極編）
Milestone25th anniversary, running 2026–20277th anniversary, December
The set codes tell you what a product is RP is the numbered regular line — the one that defines the format. EX is an extra set, usually a collaboration or a "best of" compilation. SD and BD are preconstructed decks. On the PLAY'S side, DMPP is regular and DMPX is not — which is why the current idol set does not have a number in the main sequence.

December2026 · anniversary
est. PLAY'SBigLEGEND OF PLAY'S 2027 — 7th anniversary set The anniversary product runs on its own annual track, back-to-back with the November regular set. Important for New Division: cards in an anniversary set stay ND-legal for a full year, even if the regular pack they originally came from has already rotated out.
Why the anniversary set matters more than it looks LEGEND OF PLAY'S reprints. A card whose original pack has rotated out of New Division becomes legal again if the same card appears in the anniversary product, and stays legal until the next anniversary. In practice this is the single biggest scheduled shift in the ND card pool each year, and it lands in December.

Beyond the dates

The 25th anniversary runs through 2027. Takara Tomy announced it in March 2026, and it has already produced a starter deck (DM26-SD1, April) and the current arc opener (DM26-RP1, April). Among the announced novelties: products containing cards of which only twenty-five copies exist worldwide.

PLAY'S published a full-year roadmap at the February 2026 PLAY'S Fes broadcast, covering card packs, official tournaments and offline events. The two-month set cadence has held all year, and the November-into-December double — regular set, then anniversary set — matches 2025.

Ongoing competitive structure continues regardless of the calendar: Battle Arena runs in-app qualifiers into online rounds into an offline studio final with prize money, and the Grand Master decider closes the year with Battle Arena winners only.
