Twenty-four bugs, and five of them make a card stop existing

The publisher keeps one running notice of known issues and edits it in place. On 30 September it was updated with twenty-four newly confirmed bugs — six days after set 37 arrived. One of them can lock the new story chapter. One cancels an attack made with the set's own keyword.

What the notice says

24
newly confirmed — added in this update, not yet fixed.
10
already fixed — listed as resolved.
4
still under investigation — all display problems rather than rules problems.

No fix dates are given. The notice says only that issues needing an app update can take time because of store review. This page describes the ones that connect to what this site has already written about; it does not reproduce the list.

The pattern worth naming

Five of the twenty-four end the same way. The Japanese is どのゾーンにも存在しなくなる — "ceases to exist in any zone".

Not destroyed, not removed, not shuffled away. The card is no longer in the battle zone, the graveyard, the hand, the deck, the mana zone or the shields. It is gone from the game. Five separate bugs, five different routes to the same end

They are reached by different routes — a field card moved while its destination zone is full, a creature put into play by a spell and then destroyed, gods moved after a mass summon, a spell revealed by a creature that then leaves, and the bottom card of a face-up shield stack. Different cards, different triggers, same result.

The ones that touch this site's coverage

Story · can stop the game EPISODE 37, chapter two. In that duel, when the CPU opponent casts 卍夜の降凰祭 using the 無月の門・終 ability, the game can reach a state where it cannot proceed.

EPISODE 37 is the story chapter released with the set on 24 September. It is the newest thing in the game and it can soft-lock.
Keyword · J Change Attack with a creature that has J Change, swap it for キング・ザ・セプトン7, then use that creature's on-play effect to stack a Jokers NEO evolution creature on top of it — and the attack is cancelled.

J Change is one of the keywords this site documented when the set was revealed. This is the first confirmed bug in it.
Ceases to exist · the Magic Tools field 卍 新世壊 卍 with cards stored under it, turned into a shield or returned to hand while that destination zone is already at its limit, ends up in no zone at all.

This is the field card the Magic Tools decks are built on — the family that won the Grand Prix and led the new format for its first three days.
Ceases to exist · Clanvia Clanvia appears twice. If it leaves the battle zone between its on-play ability triggering and that ability resolving, the spell it revealed ends up in no zone. A second, smaller bug leaves the opponent's mana zone display stale until the next turn begins.

What this is, and what it is not

A digital card game publishing a bug list after a set is normal. Twenty-four confirmed issues in six days is a number without much meaning on its own — there is no baseline here to compare it against, and the same notice records ten fixes.

What is worth reporting is which ones they are. The story chapter written for the set can stop. A keyword introduced by the set has a confirmed interaction that cancels your attack. And the engine card of the most-played deck family can be removed from the game by a zone-limit edge case.

None of this is a balance change Four cards had their text changed at the release maintenance; that was deliberate and announced separately. The list described here is the publisher saying things do not work as written, which is a different kind of notice and carries no compensation.
