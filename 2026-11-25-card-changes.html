PLAY’S · News · 25 Nov 2026

The restriction comes off, and six other cards change with it

The notice arrived. On 24 November at 5:00 PM JST the publisher posted news/16667, which lifts the DP殿堂 on 《インビンシブル・テクノロジー》 at the 26 November maintenance and changes six other cards in the same breath.

The account had promised that notice on 19 November and said only that the maintenance would be “in November”. The page that followed the wait is updated.

Three of the seven are cards from the pack’s own reveal run

The lifted card is one of them. 《インビンシブル・テクノロジー》 was posted on 19 November at 21:00 as the spell half of 《クリスタル・ツヴァイランサー/インビンシブル・テクノロジー》, and named one minute later as the guessing campaign’s answer. 《爆獣ボルカグレンオー》 was posted on 14 November at 21:09 as the creature half of 《爆獣ボルカグレンオー/魔弾ボルカ・ヒート》, and 《魔弾バレット・バイス》 at 21:08 the same night as the spell half of 《魔光ドラム・トレボール/魔弾バレット・バイス》. All three are in the reveal table, and the other four — 《電脳の女王 アリス》, 《魔弾ソウル・キャッチャー》, 《魔光王機デ・バウラ伯》 and 《透明妖精リリン》 — are not, checked name against the table rather than read off it.

The cards are being adjusted in the same maintenance that adds the pack.

Nothing published connects the two things. The notice gives one reason for all seven changes — ゲームバランスの適正化, bringing the game balance into line — and does not mention DMPX-04, the twinpacts, or the reveal run. A publisher changing a card in the same week it sells a card with that name on it is a schedule, and this page is not turning it into an argument.

What the changes do, read plainly

Five of the six changes are better for the player holding the card, and one is mixed. 《電脳の女王 アリス》 triples in power. 《魔弾ソウル・キャッチャー》 stops returning a random creature and lets you pick. 《魔弾バレット・バイス》 stops letting the opponent choose what to pitch. 《透明妖精リリン》 goes from discounting the first colourless creature of your turn to discounting all of them. The fifth, 《魔光王機デ・バウラ伯》, is the next paragraph. The mixed one is 《爆獣ボルカグレンオー》.

The 《魔光王機デ・バウラ伯》 change is the one that needs the glossary. It used to Search your graveyard for a spell, and now simply returns one. A Search in this game is not a tutor. What it offers, in the glossary’s words, is up to three distinct cards that meet the condition, drawn at random from those that qualify, and You choose one of what you are shown. Removing it turns a capped, partly random offer into a free pick of the whole graveyard.

One thing that looks like a change is not. 《透明妖精リリン》’s floor is reworded from 1より少なくならない to 0以下にならない — both bottom out at a cost of 1, and the real change in that line is the word 初めて going away. Separately, 《爆獣ボルカグレンオー》 is the only mixed one in the list: a cost drop from 3 to 2 with the power halved from 2000 to 1000. Whether that nets out as better or worse is not something this page decides, and the notice does not say either.

No compensation this round, and the notice does not say why

今回の調整に関し、DMポイントの数値変更による補償は行いません。

That is the whole of it. The same notice restates the standing policy in its next section:

下方修正となる場合は、当該カードを分解した時に獲得できるDMポイントの数値変更やアイテムの配布によって補償いたします。

The notice never says which of the seven it counts as a downgrade, and the two sentences are reported here as the two sentences they are. A reader can see that 《爆獣ボルカグレンオー》 loses half its power; whether the publisher classes that as 下方修正 is not stated anywhere, and this page is not deciding it.

A set leaves New Division on the same maintenance

Notice news/16642, posted at the same hour, lists the [New Division] card pool from the end of the 26 November maintenance. DMPP-33 黒夜の魔凰 drops to [All Division] only, and DMPX-04 and DMPB-08 LEGEND OF PLAY’S 2026 are added. The notice lists no AD殿堂 cards at all: なし.

So the new pack is legal in the rotating format from the moment it exists, and DMPP-33, which is legal there this morning, stops being so when Thursday’s maintenance ends. The notice gives the pool and not the reason, and no rotation rule is quoted here because the notice does not state one.
