Which collaboration they are from

The posts do not say. A notice does. news/16433, 「【ショップ】「仮想の決闘者 第6弾」コラボアイテム復刻！」, 22 October 4:00 PM, revives protects and playmats from the sixth collaboration and lists the designs on sale: 加賀美ハヤト（ゴシック）, イブラヒム, 静凛, 樋口楓, 早乙女ベリー, 仮想の決闘者 第6弾 and メラメラ・ジョーカーズ.

All five livers in this run are in that list, which places them in the sixth collaboration. Five livers are named there and all five have now appeared, the last of them on 4 November. When this page first ran, four had appeared and it said that was a count and not a forecast; the fifth was 加賀美ハヤト, the one the count left over.

The notice does not cover these cards news/16433 also opens an exchange, and the cards in it are 《スラッシュ・チャージャー》（PR） and 《メラメラ・ジョーカーズ》（PR） at 600 points, 3,000 for premium. Not one of the nine cards posted across these five nights is in that notice. The skins are not in it either. So the accessories of the sixth collaboration are documented and the skins and cards of the same collaboration are not.

What the artwork says about getting them

Every one of the five card posts carries the same banner down the right edge: 「コラボセット購入で獲得可能!!」 — obtainable by buying the Collab Set — over a caption reading 『にじさんじ』コラボカード and the card names. The 3 November image was read at full resolution; on the other four the banner is legible in the post as displayed.

That banner and the Collab Set’s documented contents do not fit together The Collab Set is the seventh collaboration’s, and this site’s calendar records that its one PR card is drawn at random from a pool of five, premium-only, taken from the 20 October notices. These nine cards are not those five.

So either the set holds more than the notices describe, or the banner means something looser than membership of that pool. Nothing published says which, and this page is not choosing. It is recorded because the same banner is at the centre of an open question on this site about a card of 栞葉るり’s — that page still ends That is still a contradiction and this page is still not resolving it. What changes is the weight of the banner: it has now appeared on nine cards that the documented pool does not contain, so it is not, on its own, evidence that a card is in it.
