An old evolution keyword, and the one line in it never printed before

DM26-EX5 went on sale today, and its two new evolution creatures both print 超無限進化. On a card you have not seen before, in a set you have not played yet, that reads like a keyword built for the occasion.

It is not. The official card database returns 50 results for that string, on set codes running back to DMR-04. What has never been printed before is the condition attached to it here — and that is checkable in one search.

What the two cards say

Mugen Ryuo Bolshack Rising NEX 《無限龍王ボルシャック・ライジングNEX》 · DM26EX5 ㊙5/㊙5, S10/S10

超無限進化：進化元のカードが２枚以上になるように、クリーチャー１体以上の上に置く。

Place it on one or more creatures, so that the cards underneath come to two or more.

Ankoku Shinraou Zero Phoenix 《暗黒神羅凰ゼロ・フェニックス》 · DM26EX5 S8/S10

超無限進化：進化元のカードが２枚以上になるように、自分のクリーチャー１体以上の上に置く。

The same line with two characters added: 自分の, your own. The first card does not say whose creatures they have to be.

The keyword is older than the set, by a lot

Searching the official card database for 超無限進化 returns 50 results. Those results carry set codes including dmr04, dmr08, dmx15, dmx19, dmx25, dmex01, dmex08, dm24bd5, dm24ex3, dm25ex2, dm25ex4, dm26ex1 and dm26ex4, as well as today’s dm26ex5.

Results, not cards. The database counts each printing separately, and several of these cards appear twice. The point the number carries is only that the keyword has a long history, not how many distinct creatures have had it.

What the condition has been, until today

The keyword itself only says stack this on something. Every card then states what it may be stacked on, and that clause is where the design lives. Across the results it has been:

A race. 《悪魔世界ワルドバロム》 takes デーモン・コマンド・クリーチャー１体以上の上に置く。 《龍覇龍 デッドマン》 takes ドラグナー・クリーチャー１体以上の上に置く。

A total cost. 《切札連結 バロXGXディアス》 reads コストの合計が20以上になるように、進化ではない、コマンドまたはドラゴンいずれかのクリーチャー１体以上の上に置く。 《超時空ストームG・XX》 uses the same threshold against サイキック・クリーチャー.

A race, your own only. 《無限龍ファタル・スパイラル》 takes 自分のオリジンまたはドラゴン; 《勝利皇帝Guy-R》 takes 自分のアウトレイジ.

Nothing at all. 《無限銀河ジ・エンド・オブ・ユニバース》 reads, on its dm26ex1 printing, simply クリーチャー１体以上の上に置く。

That last card is worth a second look, because its two printings in the database do not read the same. The dmex01 entry says 自分のクリーチャー１体以上の上に置く。 — your own — and the dm26ex1 entry drops those two characters. This page is reporting what the two entries say, and is not claiming to know which direction the change ran or why.

The line that has never been printed

None of the conditions above counts cards. They count races, civilisations, or added-up cost. The two new cards count the cards that end up underneath, and they do it with the phrase 進化元のカードが.

Searching the whole database for that phrase returns three results. They are dm26ex5-SEC005, dm26ex5-S008 and dm26ex5-S010 — the two cards above, one of them in two printings. Nothing else in the game has ever carried it.

So the thing worth noticing about this set is not a new keyword. It is an old keyword given a kind of condition it has never had: a minimum on the stack itself, which means these two cannot be dropped onto a single bare creature the way an unconditioned one can.

The set’s third card with the keyword is a reprint

《氷結龍 ダイヤモンド・クレバス》 appears in DM26-EX5 at G10/G20 and also carries 超無限進化 — in its ・Ω form. Its text is identical to the dm24bd5 printing of the same card, down to the clause: 超無限進化・Ω：スノーフェアリー・クリーチャーを１体以上、自分の墓地、マナゾーンまたはバトルゾーンから選び、このクリーチャーをその上に重ねつつ出す。 It is in the set, but it is not new, and it is not part of the change described above.

超無限進化・Ω is its own printed form and behaves differently — it pulls the creature underneath out of the graveyard, mana zone or battle zone rather than requiring one already in play. 《「無情」の極 シャングリラ》 and 《「終」の極 イギー・スペシャルズ》 carry it too. A search for the shorter string finds both forms, which is worth knowing before reading a count off it.

How this was checked

The official card search gives no indication when it has failed to apply a keyword: a dead search and a real zero return the same page. Every count here was taken with a control. ボルシャック returned 452 the same way the real searches were run, so a zero means a zero. And because 超無限進化 is a compound, the engine was tested for word-splitting with 無限究極進化, a string that should not exist: it returned 0, which is what establishes that the 50 are matches on the whole phrase rather than on its parts.

The set released today, and these cards were not in the database yesterday: on 6 November ミザリィ and 無限龍王 both returned 0, and 超無限進化 returned 46 rather than today’s 50. This site covered the set’s announcement in August and described it then as a set where it runs the transfer backwards.
