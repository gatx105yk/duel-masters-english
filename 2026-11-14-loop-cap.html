PLAY’S · News · 14 Nov 2026

A card left on the board and given a counter instead

On 12 November at 3:00 PM JST the publisher posted a notice that does something it has not done in the time this site has been watching: it found a card whose ability could be made to apply without end, and instead of changing the card, it put a number on how many times the ability may apply in a turn.

What the notice says

一部カードの能力が、特定の条件や状況により際限なく（ループ）適用される状況を確認しております。ループ適用への対策として、対象カード能力の適用を一定回数に制限いたしました。

An ability was found that, under certain conditions, applies without end — the notice uses the word ループ for it — and the countermeasure is to limit how many times it may apply.

各プレイヤーの1ターン中、効果の適用を9回までとする。
※１0回目以降は、効果を発動させた場合でも適用されません。

Nine per player per turn.

From the tenth onward the effect simply does not apply, even though it still triggers. The notice is explicit about that: the thing fires and then does nothing.

What is capped

One card, and one of its abilities. The notice names them as:

・≪傀儡が来る！≫
■自分の墓地からクリーチャー1枚をバトルゾーンに出す。

Put one creature from your graveyard into the battle zone. Nothing about the card’s cost, its text or its availability has changed — the notice announces no revision to the card at all. The limit sits outside it, as a count the game keeps.

The same name on paper is half of a twinpact

Searching the official card database for that name returns exactly one card: 《阿修羅ンチュラ/傀儡が来る！》, from DMRP-08 双極篇. It is a twinpact — a creature on one half and a spell on the other — and 傀儡が来る！ is the spell half. Its paper text reads:

クリーチャーを１体、自分の墓地からバトルゾーンに出す。

That is the same effect in different words from the ones the notice uses. The notice writes 自分の墓地からクリーチャー1枚を; the card writes クリーチャーを１体、自分の墓地から, with a full-width １ where the notice has a half-width 1. This site records differences like that rather than smoothing them, because they are the kind of thing that makes a search for one text fail to find the other.

The creature half carries スマッシュ・バースト, which lets the spell half be cast, free, when the creature attacks. This page is not going to explain the loop — the publisher did not describe it, and reconstructing one from a card text would be guessing at the thing the notice deliberately does not spell out.

One day later, a twinpact announcement

On 13 November at 20:00, the day after the cap, the same account posted a teaser for 『PLAY’S TWINPACT PROJECT PACK』. At the time of writing that post has 587,041 views and 3,196 reposts, which is far above anything else this site has measured on the account.

The two are reported here in the order they happened and are not being connected. Nothing published says they are related, a capped card and a coming pack are not the same subject, and one following the other by a day is not evidence of anything.

It is also the first notice in thirteen days

The news index carried nothing new between 30 October and this notice on 12 November — through a collaboration running the whole time, a paper set release and a tournament round. This is what broke that run.

Sources The notice: dmps.takaratomy.co.jp/news/16622, posted 2026年11月12日（木）3:00 PM, read 14 November 2026. The paper card: its own page in the official card database at dm.takaratomy.co.jp/card/, the single result for a search on that name. The teaser post and its figures: the official account’s own accessibility labels, read 14 November.

The Japanese above is quoted as the sources write it, including the card-name brackets ≪ ≫ the notice uses, which are not the 《 》 used elsewhere on the same site, and including ※１0回目, where the publisher's own text mixes a full-width １ with a half-width 0. Neither is a transcription error on this page.

This page makes no claim about paper play. The cap is a change to the digital game. Nothing published says the paper card is affected, and the card database shows no revision to it.
