PRECIOUS IDOL FESTIVAL: what the official account has actually said

DMPX-03 went live on 23 July 2026 and is the set the current New Division metagame is built on. Everything below is summarised from the official site and the @dmps_info account, with the original posts attached as sources. If the embeds do not load, the Japanese text is written out underneath each one.

The set at a glance

Set codeDMPX-03
TitlePRECIOUS IDOL FESTIVAL （プレシャス アイドル フェスティバル）
Live23 July 2026, after maintenance
TypeNot a regular pack. See below.
New rarityPLAY'S CHARACTER CARD (PCC)
The X in DMPX-03 matters. Official copy for the login campaign runs "until the next regular card pack release," which places DMPX-03 outside the numbered regular line. If you have been counting sets and wondering where set 37 went, this is why — it has not arrived yet. DMPX is a separate track.

What is in it

Five idol concepts, built so that collecting one idol's cards produces a working deck without needing to understand the wider format. A new rarity tier, PLAY'S CHARACTER CARD, sits at the same generation and disenchant cost as Victory, Legend and Master Rare. Five character-sung tracks were added as a new BGM set called STARS, unlocked by obtaining the corresponding Secrets.

Announcements, most recent first

13 AUG 2026

Two Secret-rarity cards revealed in their premium versions: Chōkaitaku Yōsei Ajisai! 《超開拓妖精アジサイ！》 and Shugo Suiryū Hydrangea 《守護水龍 ハイドランジア》. Premium versions of Secrets carry a printed idol signature across the art.

Ajisai is worth noting for competitive players: Light/Water/Nature Ajisai! is currently sitting in Tier A of the New Division list.

Where this set left the metagame

Two weeks after release, Kamigame's New Division tier list had Lionel decks holding three of five Tier S slots, with four of the five having climbed that week. Ajisai! — one of the cards revealed in the 13 August post above — anchors a Tier A deck.
