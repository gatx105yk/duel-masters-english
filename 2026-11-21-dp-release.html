PLAY’S · News · 21 Nov 2026

A restricted card is being unrestricted, and the notice is not out yet

On 19 November at 21:02 JST the official account said that 《インビンシブル・テクノロジー》 will come off DP殿堂 — the digital game’s restriction list — in a maintenance planned for November.

A November maintenance now has a date — 26 November — and the notice that gives it does not mention this card. It is the only November maintenance this page has a notice for, which is not the same as the only one there was. Notice 16640 went up on 23 November and the words 殿堂 and インビンシブル do not appear in it. That notice has its own page.

A maintenance planned for November 2026; the restriction lifted; and details to follow in a notice to be published later. No date is given for either the maintenance or the notice.

One minute earlier, the same account had named that card as the answer to a competition.

This page is not telling you the three are causally connected. Nothing published says the restriction is being lifted because the card is becoming half of a twinpact, and a publisher posting three things in three minutes is a publishing schedule, not an argument. The twinpact run has its own page.

What the list is, and what paper has to do with it

DP殿堂 is the digital game’s own restriction list. As the glossary here puts it, PLAY'S has its own restriction list, separate from paper. A card on it may be included once per deck; the calendar’s tournament entries repeat that condition for each format that uses it.

Paper has its own lists and its own word for this. When this site covered the August 2026 paper revision, the tier that means four copies again was 解除 — the same word in the post above. The two games’ lists are not the same list, and a card’s standing in one says nothing about its standing in the other.

It is here because the name is shared and because a reader looking the card up will find two different cards under it.

What to watch for

The post promises a notice, and as of 12:13 JST on 24 November no notice about the restriction has appeared. What has appeared is a November maintenance notice, 16640 of 23 November, which dates the maintenance to 26 November, 10:00 to 16:00 JST and does not name this card or the list it is on anywhere in its 20,994 characters of page text or its 81,805-character source.

That is not evidence the lift has been dropped. The notice carries ◆一部機能改修および調整対応 and ◆データ更新 without itemising either, so the change could be inside one of those and simply unnamed. Nothing published says which, and this page is not deciding it. The thing to watch is whether a notice naming the card appears before, during or after the 26th — or at all.
