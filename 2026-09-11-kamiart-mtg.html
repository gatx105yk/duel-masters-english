They are doing it again, and this time Magic is posting too

Two premium art sets built around Magic: The Gathering went on sale for pre-order yesterday evening. Both official accounts revealed cards in turn — Duel Masters showing one set, Magic showing the other, pointing at each other across the timeline.

The detail worth stopping on: a Magic artist drew one of the Duel Masters cards.

What was announced

Kami Art Kizuna — 神アート絆 — is Takara Tomy's premium art line: made-to-order product with new artwork on existing cards, sold direct rather than through shops. Two sets, released together.

Why the artist credit matters

The earlier crossover put Magic cards into Duel Masters — Black Lotus, Emrakul, Nicol Bolas — reprinted in frames imitating Magic's old border. That was one game visiting another.

This is not that. These are Duel Masters cards, with Duel Masters names and Duel Masters rules text, drawn by someone who works for Magic. The distinction the announcement makes, stated plainly

The traffic has been going both ways for a while. In 2023, artwork commissioned for the Duel Masters versions of Magic cards was brought back into Magic as Friday Night Magic promos — Serra Angel and Pouncing Jaguar among them, wearing art that was made for the other game.

Wizards of the Coast designed Duel Masters in 2002 and the two games have shared material ever since. What is new here is both companies running the reveal as a joint operation, with each account handling one set and directing readers to the other.

For English speakers

You cannot buy this Takara Tomy Mall ships within Japan. Made-to-order means there is no secondary supply beyond what people ordered during the window, and the window is already open.

This is listed here because it is the kind of thing a Magic player would want to know exists, not because it is actionable.

The cards themselves are ordinary Duel Masters cards with premium art — no new mechanics, no new names. What you are paying for is the artwork and the production quality.
