PLAY’S · News · 24 Nov 2026

The pack arrives in a maintenance window, and the restriction still has no notice

DMPX-04 『PLAY'S TWINPACT PROJECT PACK』 lands on Thursday 26 November, in a maintenance running 10:00 to 16:00 JST. The date was announced on X on 21 November, in the middle of a tournament broadcast; the notice that carries it went up two days later.

The same notice does two other things. It cuts Quick Pick’s card pool from fourteen products to five mid-season. And it says nothing at all about 《インビンシブル・テクノロジー》 coming off DP殿堂, which the account announced on 19 November and said a notice would explain.

The date, twice

21 November, 22:13 JST, posted during the reveal run’s own broadcast:

【リリース日解禁！】
『PLAY'S TWINPACT PROJECT PACK』
11月26日（木）リリース！

23 November, 5:00 PM, in notice 16640, メンテナンスのお知らせ（Ver.4.9.0アップデート）:

◆メンテナンス期間
2026年11月26日（木）10:00　～　11月26日（木）16:00　予定

And the first item under メンテナンス内容:

カードパック『PLAY'S TWINPACT PROJECT PACK』追加対応

So the pack is not released on a schedule of its own — it is added by the maintenance, along with an app update to Ver.4.9.0. The notice warns that duels interrupted by the maintenance do not count, and asks players to stop about fifteen minutes before it starts.

Quick Pick keeps four of its fourteen products

The notice’s third item is a change to the season already running, and it is a large one:

第61回クイック・ピックにて、提示されるカードの変更対応

The list that follows, under 【メンテナンス終了後から提示されるカード】, is five products:

After the maintenanceBefore it
DMPP-36 極罠と聖剣Fourteen products — DMPP-33 to DMPP-37, DMPX-03, DMPS-15 to 17, DMPD-18 to 20, DMPB-08 and 09

DMPX-04 PLAY'S TWINPACT PROJECT PACK
DMPB-08 LEGEND OF PLAY'S 2026

Ten products drop out, four stay and the new pack goes in — four of the five listed after the maintenance were in the pool before it, and DMPX-04 is the one that was not. The season runs to 15 December, so this lands in the middle of it — the calendar entry for Quick Pick 61 recorded the fourteen-product pool as the one the season opened with, carried over from a mid-season change on 22 October. That makes two pool changes inside one season.

The notice repeats the two conditions the season started with: some cards are not selected, and the round that offers only 「S・トリガー」 cards draws from すべてのカードプール. What that phrase is measured against is still not stated, and with the product list now cut to five it matters more than it did.

What the notice does not say

No mention of DP殿堂. None of 《インビンシブル・テクノロジー》.

On 19 November the account said the spell would come off the restriction list 2026年11月に実施予定のメンテナンスにおいて — in a maintenance planned for November — and that 詳しくは後日公開予定のお知らせをご確認ください. This is a November maintenance notice, and the words 殿堂 and インビンシブル do not appear in it once. The whole page’s text was searched for both — 20,994 characters, of which the notice itself is 1,427 and the rest is the site around it — and so was the 81,805-character page source. Neither word is in either, re-measured at 12:16 JST on 24 November.

That does not mean the lift is off. The notice lists ◆一部機能改修および調整対応 and ◆データ更新 without itemising either, so the change could sit inside one of those lines and simply not be named. Nothing published says which, and this page is not deciding it. What can be said is that the promised notice has not appeared as a notice about the restriction, five days after the post. The 26th is the only November maintenance this page has a notice for, and that is not the same as the only one there was: whether the month held an earlier maintenance, and whether the lift could already have happened in it, was not checked here.

A third notice, and a date for the next maintenance after this one

16632, 【シティバトル】内容更新について, went up in the same 5:00 PM slot on 23 November. City Battle is updated at the same maintenance, and its window is given as:

2026年11月26日（木）メンテナンス終了後　～
2026年12月17日（木）メンテナンス開始

So there is a maintenance on 17 December too, named here only as the end of this window. No time is given for it and nothing says what is in it.

Both characters appear elsewhere on both pages, and no count is given for that — the total depends on whether the site’s navigation and page title are counted, so it is a number that does not reproduce. The counts above are of the full string PLAY'S TWINPACT PROJECT PACK, which does. That is four this page can point at and not a count of every one on the site — no search was run for the rest.
